# Code of conduct

Hildegard follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind, assume good faith, criticise work rather than people, and keep harassment of any kind out.

Report anything that breaks it to donovan@dbaney.com. Reports are kept private, and the maintainer decides what
happens, from a word in private to a ban.
