Logos: the burning-egg mark, the wordmark and both lockups.

- `*-vellum.svg`: on ink, `canvas` (dark) and `viridian-deep`. Ink is vellum `#f4eedd`, the lens and flame-dot gold leaf `#e2bf6a`.
- `*-green.svg`: on vellum and `canvas` (light). Ink is viriditas `#1f5a3d`, gold `#a67710`.
- `wordmark-ink.svg`: near-black `#1a1914` ink for print on paper.
- `mark-mono-ink.svg`, `mark-mono-vellum.svg`: one-colour marks for 20px and below, embossing, and single-ink print.
- Horizontal lockup: the H stems stand on the wordmark baseline and the flame rises above the ascenders. Stacked lockup for square spaces and splash screens.
