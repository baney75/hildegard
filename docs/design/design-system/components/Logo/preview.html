On viriditas · one-colour mark at right

The mark

Two strokes of a 42° broad nib write the cosmic egg of the Scivias and the stems of an H. The left stroke rises into a hairline tongue of flame, the fiery light of her visions. A gold lens crosses the counter: lux vivens, the Living Light.

Clear space and size

Keep clear space equal to the lens width on every side. Smallest mark: 16px tall on screen, 6mm in print. Below 20px use the one-colour mark so the lens does not fill in.

Never

Never redraw the strokes with a monoline pen, outline the mark, set the wordmark in another face, recolour the lens anything but gold, or put the mark on a busy image without a solid ground.
