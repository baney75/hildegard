Dear Grace Lutheran Family,

Many of you have noticed the buckets. When rain comes, water finds its way through our sanctuary roof and drips onto the north pews. We have patched it, we have watched it, and we have prayed over it. It is time to fix it properly.

Hauge Roofing has submitted a bid of $64,200 to replace the sanctuary roof. Our building fund holds $21,500 toward that cost. That leaves $42,700 to raise.

At its February meeting, the church council voted 7 to 1 to ask the congregation to help close that gap by Easter. We know this is a significant request. We also know that this building has held our worship, our baptisms, our weddings, and our funerals for generations, and that many of you have given faithfully to keep it standing. We are asking you to help once more.

Here is how you can help:

**Pancake Breakfast, Sunday, March 8.** Join us for a breakfast fellowship with all proceeds going directly to the roof fund. Bring your friends and your appetite.

**Matching Gift Challenge.** Evelyn Marsh has offered a generous gift of $5,000 if our congregation and friends together give $20,000 in other gifts by March 31. Every dollar you give between now and then counts twice toward this goal, so please consider what you can give.

**Give directly.** Gifts can be placed in the offering plate marked "Roof," mailed to the church office, or given online through our website. Please note "Roof Fund" on your gift.

**Congregation meeting, Sunday, February 16, after worship.** We will gather to review the Hauge Roofing bid, answer your questions, and talk together about next steps. Please plan to attend if you can.

We do not take this lightly, and we do not ask it lightly. But we believe God has provided for this house of worship through the generosity of this congregation, and we trust that same generosity will carry us through this need. Please pray for our church, for our council, and for the work ahead.

In Christ,

Pastor Greg Hollis
Grace Lutheran Church
Ames, Iowa
