**Subject: Our Sanctuary Roof: A Request and an Invitation**

Dear Grace family,

Many of you have noticed the water. When it rains, the roof leaks over the north pews, and the problem is getting worse. Our council has spent the last several months looking into it, and we have now received a firm bid to make the repair properly.

Hauge Roofing has bid $64,200 to replace the sanctuary roof. Our building fund holds $21,500 toward that cost, which leaves $42,700 still to raise. The church council voted 7 to 1 to ask the congregation to help cover that amount by Easter. The one dissenting vote came from a member who wanted more time to explore other options before committing, and we take that concern seriously. We believe the leak will not wait, and neither should we.

Here is how you can help:

**A Pancake Breakfast on Sunday, March 8.** All proceeds will go directly to the roof. Please plan to join us, bring your friends and neighbors, and come hungry.

**A Matching Gift Challenge.** Evelyn Marsh has offered a gift of $5,000 to match the roof fund, but only if we reach $20,000 in other gifts by March 31. Every gift you give, whether it is a single dollar or a larger pledge, moves us closer to that goal and unlocks Evelyn's generosity. Please prayerfully consider what you can give, and give as soon as you are able.

**A Congregation Meeting on Sunday, February 16, after worship.** We will review the bid, answer your questions, and talk together about the plan. I encourage every member to attend. This is our church, and this decision belongs to all of us.

I know that asking for money is never comfortable, and I know many of you have already given generously to the many needs of this congregation over the years. I am grateful for every one of you. A roof protects more than the building. It protects the place where we gather to hear God's Word, receive the sacraments, and care for one another. Let us take care of it together.

Please pray for this effort, for our council, and for the generous hearts God has already placed among us. If you have questions before the meeting, please contact me or any council member.

In Christ,

Pastor Greg Hollis
Grace Lutheran Church
Ames, Iowa
