# This Week on the Farm

Good morning, members. The light is low and golden now, and the field has gone quiet in the way it does every November. The beds that were full of tomatoes and beans in August are mostly turned under, and what's left is the hardy stuff: kale that sweetens after a frost, leeks, carrots pulled from the cold ground, and the winter squash we've been curing in the barn.

**What's in your share this week**

This week's box leans toward the storage season. Expect a mix of:

- **Winter squash**, a good reason to try the roasted-squash-and-sage recipe you've been saving
- **Kale or chard**, which tastes better after the cold snap, so don't be shy about cooking it
- **Carrots and beets**, sweeter than they were a month ago
- **Leeks or onions**, great for the soup pot as the weather turns
- **Something surprising**, because the field always has a few more things to show us

Every box is a little different, and that's the point. If you're unsure how to use something, reply to this email or find us at pickup and we'll talk you through it.

**From the field**

We finished planting garlic last week, and the cloves are tucked in under mulch, waiting for their long winter nap. Next summer's first bulbs depend on this work today, so it's a quiet kind of faith. The cover crop of crimson clover and rye is coming up in the empty beds too, holding the soil and feeding it for spring.

The crew is also cleaning and organizing the barn for the winter months. Thank you to everyone who has returned crates, jars, and bags this season. You keep this operation running in ways that don't show up on a receipt.

**Winter shares are open**

If you'd like fresh produce through the cold months, our winter shares are now open. They're a good fit for anyone who loved the summer boxes and wants to keep the connection going. Members who sign up early help us plan planting and storage for the coming year, so the sooner you join, the more it helps us. Details and sign-up are on our website, and we're happy to answer questions by email.

**A few reminders**

- Please bring your own bags or crates. We're running low on spares.
- If you're going out of town, let us know ahead of pickup so your share can go to someone who will use it.
- Share your favorite recipe with the group. Some of our best ideas come from members who have been cooking the same vegetables for years.

Thank you for eating with us, for showing up in the cold, and for being part of this farm. We'll see you at pickup.

With gratitude from the field,

*The farm crew*
