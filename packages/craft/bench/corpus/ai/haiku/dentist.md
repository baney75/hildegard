# We're Moving! Okafor Family Dentistry Is Relocating to Stellhorn Road

After more than two decades at 1420 N. Wells Street, Okafor Family Dentistry is moving to a new home at **6115 Stellhorn Road** in Fort Wayne. We're excited to take this next step, and we want to make sure you know what to expect.

Dr. Samuel Okafor has practiced in Fort Wayne since 2004, and our patients have always been at the heart of this practice. This move is about giving our patients and our team more room to provide the care you deserve.

**What's changing**

- **More treatment space:** The new office has six treatment chairs, up from four, so we can see patients with less waiting.
- **Accessible entrance:** The new office has a wheelchair-accessible entrance.
- **Ample parking:** Parking for 30 cars is available at the new location.

**Important dates**

- **January 5–9:** Our office will be closed while we move.
- **January 12:** We open at our new location on Stellhorn Road.

**Your appointments**

If you have an appointment scheduled after January 12, it stays exactly as booked. You don't need to reschedule or do anything else. We'll see you at 6115 Stellhorn Road.

**Staying in touch**

Our phone number is not changing. You can still reach us at **(260) 555-0198**, including during the move.

Thank you for trusting us with your smiles over the years. We look forward to welcoming you to our new office.
