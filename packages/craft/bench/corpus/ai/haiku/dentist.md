# Okafor Family Dentistry Is Moving to a New Home on Stellhorn Road

After more than two decades on North Wells Street, Okafor Family Dentistry is packing up and moving to a new location in Fort Wayne. Our new office is at **6115 Stellhorn Road**, and we will open there on **January 12**.

Dr. Samuel Okafor has practiced here since 2004, and he has cared for generations of families in that time. The move gives us room to grow and to serve you better.

**What's changing:**

- **New address:** 6115 Stellhorn Road, Fort Wayne
- **Old address:** 1420 N. Wells Street
- **More treatment space:** We are expanding from 4 chairs to 6, so you should spend less time waiting.
- **Accessible entrance:** The new office has a wheelchair-accessible entrance.
- **Ample parking:** The new lot has space for 30 cars.

**Closure dates:** We will be closed **January 5 through January 9** while we move. Our office will reopen at the new location on January 12.

**Your appointments:** Any appointment scheduled on or after January 12 stays as booked. You do not need to reschedule. If you have an appointment during the closure dates, our team will contact you to arrange a new time.

**Our phone number stays the same:** You can still reach us at **(260) 555-0198**.

We are grateful for your trust over the years, and we look forward to seeing you at our new home on Stellhorn Road.
