# About Petal & Pine

Petal & Pine has been part of Center Street in Rutland since 2012. We opened in a former hardware store, and the old wooden floors and tall front windows still shape how we arrange flowers. Our shop is small and busy, and most days you'll find one of our two full-time designers, Josh and Leah, working at the counter or at the bench in back.

I'm Nadine Fournier, the owner. I started Petal & Pine because I wanted a florist who paid close attention to what each flower is and where it comes from.

Much of what we use is grown on our two acres in Pittsford, where we raise dahlias and zinnias. For everything else, we buy from a co-op in Brattleboro, which lets us know the growers and how their flowers were handled. We don't sell artificial flowers. If the flowers aren't alive, they aren't ours to sell.

**What we do**

- **Everyday arrangements and gifts.** Walk in, call, or order online. We'll make something to suit the occasion and the room it's going into.
- **Weddings.** We book weddings about nine months in advance, so reach out early. We'll talk through your colors, your venue, and the flowers in season on your date.
- **Funerals and sympathy.** Last year we made 1,400 funeral arrangements. We know these orders often come with little notice and a great deal of feeling, and we treat each one with the same care.
- **Delivery.** We deliver within 15 miles of the shop on Tuesdays through Saturdays for $12.

At holidays, we add three part-time designers to the team, so the shop is fuller and busier than usual. If you're ordering for a holiday, placing your order early helps us get you exactly what you asked for.

Stop by Center Street whenever you're in Rutland. We'd be glad to see you.
