# About Petal & Pine

Petal & Pine opened in 2012 in a former hardware store on Center Street in Rutland, Vermont. We kept the old wooden floors and the high shelves, and we fill them with flowers.

Our studio has two full-time designers, Josh and Leah, who create every arrangement we make. At the holidays we add three part-time helpers, so the shop stays busy when everyone wants flowers at once.

Much of what we sell is grown close to home. We raise dahlias and zinnias on two acres in Pittsford, and we cut them ourselves through the growing season. For everything else, we buy from a co-op in Brattleboro, so we can keep our selection fresh and seasonal.

We design for weddings, and we recommend booking about nine months ahead, especially for summer and fall dates. We also design for funerals and memorials. Last year we created 1,400 funeral arrangements, and we treat each one with the same care and attention.

We deliver within 15 miles of Rutland, Tuesday through Saturday, for a flat fee of $12.

We don't sell artificial flowers. Every bloom in our shop is real, and we think that's what makes a bouquet worth giving.

Thank you for supporting a small, local shop. We look forward to helping you find the right flowers for your next occasion.

Nadine Fournier
Owner, Petal & Pine
