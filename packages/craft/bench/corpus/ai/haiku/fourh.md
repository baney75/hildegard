# Clover Creek Champions November Newsletter

Greetings, Clover Creek families! November is a month of growth, achievement, and community, and we have plenty to celebrate.

**Membership Is Growing.** We welcomed six new members this year, bringing our total to 23, up from 17 last year. Welcome to every new family! We're glad you're here, and we look forward to watching you learn and grow alongside the rest of the club.

**Hampshire Gilt Earns Reserve Grand Champion.** At the Marshall County Fair in July, Hannah Yoder's Hampshire gilt took home reserve grand champion. Congratulations, Hannah, on a outstanding showing. Your hard work and dedication to your animal paid off, and we're proud of you.

**Pecan Fundraiser a Success.** Thanks to every member and family who helped out this fall, the club sold 410 pounds of pecans and cleared $1,180. That money pays for our spring trip to the state fairgrounds, so every pound sold brings us closer to a great experience for the whole club. Thank you for your support!

**December Meeting Location and Time Change.** Please note that our December meeting will be held on **Tuesday, December 9, at 7 p.m. at the Bremen Public Library**. The fire station hall is being repainted, so we're moving to the library for the month. Mark your calendars and we'll see you there!

**Volunteers Needed for State Fair Trip.** We need **two parent volunteers** to drive for the state fair trip this spring. If you can help, please contact a club leader at your earliest convenience. Thank you for helping make this trip possible for our members!

Keep up the great work, Champions. We'll see you at the December meeting!
