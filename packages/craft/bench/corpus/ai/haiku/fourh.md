# Clover Creek Champions Newsletter: November 2026

Friends and families of the Clover Creek Champions,

We're proud to report that our club grew this year. We now have **23 members**, up from 17 last year. Please join us in welcoming our new members and their families.

## Marshall County Fair Results

This July, Hannah Yoder's Hampshire gilt earned **reserve grand champion** at the Marshall County Fair. Hannah's hard work with her animal all year paid off in the show ring. Congratulations, Hannah!

## Fall Pecan Fundraiser

Thanks to everyone who helped with our fall pecan fundraiser, we sold **410 pounds of pecans** and cleared **$1,180**. That money pays for our spring trip to the state fairgrounds, so every pound sold helps send our members on this trip. Thank you to all our members, families, and customers for your support.

## Volunteers Needed for the State Fair Trip

We need **two parent volunteers** to drive for the spring state fair trip. If you can help, please contact a club leader soon so we can finalize our travel plans.

## December Meeting Location and Time

Please note that our **December meeting has moved** because the fire station hall is being repainted. We will meet at the **Bremen Public Library** on **December 9 at 7 p.m.** We look forward to seeing you there.

Thank you for another great year. We're excited for what's ahead!

Clover Creek Champions
Bremen, Indiana
