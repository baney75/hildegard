# What to Bring for a Fall Steelhead Float on the Pere Marquette

The Pere Marquette in fall is one of the best places in the Midwest to chase steelhead. The fish are moving upriver, the crowds of summer are gone, and the trees along the banks are turning. It is also cold, and the right gear makes the difference between a great day and a miserable one. Here is what to pack for a float trip with Big Two-Hearted Outfitters.

## What We Provide

You don't need to buy a rod or reel to fish with us. We supply the rods, reels, and flies, and we bring the drift boat. Everything you need to hook a steelhead is in the boat.

## What to Bring

**Breathable waders.** Water runs 38 to 46 degrees from October through early December, so waders are essential. Choose breathable models. They keep you dry and let you move comfortably through a long day of casting and rowing.

**Wading boots without felt soles.** Michigan restricts felt-soled footwear on some rivers to limit the spread of invasive species, and we won't allow felt in our boat. Bring boots with rubber or other approved soles. If you're unsure whether your boots comply, ask us before you arrive.

**Layers, and no cotton.** Dress in layers you can add or remove as the day warms or cools. Start with a synthetic or wool base layer, add an insulating mid-layer such as fleece, and finish with a waterproof, windproof outer shell. Cotton holds water and pulls heat from your body, so leave cotton t-shirts, hoodies, and jeans at home. Bring a warm hat and gloves that still let you handle line.

**A Michigan fishing license with an all-species endorsement.** This is required. Purchase it before your trip and have it with you on the water.

**Polarized sunglasses.** They cut glare on the water so you can see structure, fish, and the occasional steelhead rolling near the surface.

**Water and a snack.** You'll be on the river for much of the day. Pack something warm to drink and food that's easy to eat with cold hands.

## The Day

We launch at 7 a.m. from the Gleason's Landing access. Arrive a little early so we can get organized, check gear, and get on the water on time. Morning is when the cold is sharpest, so the layers you packed will earn their keep.
