# Our Story

Becker Hardware has been on Hastings' main street since 1955, when my grandfather, Walt Becker, borrowed $2,000 and opened a store with a simple idea: if you need something to fix something, we should have it, and we should be able to tell you how to use it.

Seventy-some years later, that idea hasn't changed. We still sell paint and plumbing parts. We still keep fasteners in bins so you can buy exactly the number of screws you need, sold by the pound. We still cut keys and replace glass. If you've lived in Hastings for a while, you've probably walked out of here with something you came in for and something you didn't know you needed.

In 1982, my dad added the garden center. Anyone who has bought a tomato plant or a bag of potting soil from us in spring knows that corner of the store. It has grown along with the town.

In 2019, the flood came through and put four feet of water in our basement. We lost a lot that spring, and we were not sure how we would reopen. Then the neighbors started showing up, many of them customers, some of them people we had never met, with shop vacs, mops, and buckets. They cleared out the mud, hauled out what was ruined, and didn't ask for anything in return. We reopened because of them. We have never forgotten it.

Today we have 11 employees. The longest-serving is Doris, who has worked our register for 31 years. If you have shopped here, she has probably helped you, and she likely remembers what you bought last time. That kind of memory is a big part of what we are.

We are not trying to beat the big box stores on price. They have their place, and sometimes that place is exactly what you need. What we offer is different: people who know the products, a store where you can get the right part the first time, and a team that will take the time to help you figure out the project in front of you.

We're open Monday through Saturday, 7:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Stop by, say hello, and let us help.

Ray Becker
Becker Hardware, Hastings, Nebraska
