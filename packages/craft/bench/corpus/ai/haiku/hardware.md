# Our Story

Becker Hardware has been on the same corner in Hastings, Nebraska, since 1955. My grandfather, Walt Becker, opened the doors that year with a loan of $2,000 and a simple idea: if you need something to fix something, we should have it, and we should be able to tell you how to use it.

Nearly seventy years later, that idea hasn't changed much. We still sell paint, plumbing supplies, and fasteners by the pound, because sometimes you need exactly eleven of a bolt and not a blister pack of twenty. We cut keys and replace glass. We'll spend time with you at the counter figuring out what you actually need, even if it's not the most expensive version on the shelf.

In 1982, my dad added the garden center, and it's grown into one of the things our customers look forward to most each spring. Seedlings, soil, tools, and a lot of advice from people who have been growing things in Nebraska soil for a long time.

In 2019, the flood came through and put four feet of water in our basement. We lost a lot. What we didn't lose was the community. Neighbors showed up with shop vacs, buckets, and dehumidifiers before we'd even asked for help. People we had served for years turned into people who showed up for us. We still think about that, and we still feel it every time someone walks through the door.

Today we have eleven employees. Among them is Doris, who has worked our register for thirty-one years. If you've shopped here, there's a good chance she's already helped you, and probably remembered your name.

We are not trying to beat the big box stores on price. They have buying power we can't match, and we respect what they do. What we offer instead is knowing what's in stock, knowing how to help, and knowing you by name. We'd rather earn your business one trip at a time.

Stop by. We're open Monday through Saturday, 7:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. We'd be glad to see you.
