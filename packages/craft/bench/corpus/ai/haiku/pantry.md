# Cedar Falls Community Pantry: October Update

Our pantry served **412 families in October**, up from 331 families in the same month last year. Of those families, **61 were visiting us for the first time**. We're grateful to everyone in Cedar Falls who helps us meet this need.

**Thank you, Hy-Vee.** The store donated **1,800 pounds of fresh produce**, which went straight to families on our shelves and tables. Their generosity made a real difference this month.

**What we need right now**

- **Peanut butter**
- **Canned chicken**
- **Diapers**, sizes 4 and 5

Donations of these items are especially welcome. If you're shopping this week, a few extra cans or jars would help more than you might think.

**We need two more drivers for Friday pickups.** If you have a few hours a week and a reliable vehicle, please reach out. Our Friday routes keep food moving from donors to the families who depend on it, and we can't keep them running without more volunteers.

**Lions Club Food Drive: November 22.** Mark your calendar and plan to donate or volunteer. Watch for details on drop-off locations.
