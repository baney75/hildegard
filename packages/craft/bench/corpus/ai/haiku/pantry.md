# Cedar Falls Community Pantry Serves Record 412 Families in October

October was a busy month at the Cedar Falls Community Pantry. We served **412 families**, up from 331 at this time last year, and **61 of them were new to the pantry**. Every one of those visits is a neighbor who needed a hand, and we are grateful to be able to offer it.

**A big thank-you to Hy-Vee**, which donated **1,800 pounds of fresh produce** this month. That donation put fruits and vegetables into many family kitchens and stretched our shelves further than we could have on our own.
