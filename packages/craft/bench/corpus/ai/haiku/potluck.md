**Harvest Potluck: Sunday, October 26**

Please join us for our Harvest Potluck this Sunday, October 26, in the parish hall, immediately following the 10:30 Mass.

Father Tom will bless the food before we eat. The Knights of Columbus will be smoking two briskets, so come hungry.

Last year 140 of you joined us, and we ran out of plates by noon. Come early, and bring your appetite.

**Please bring a dish to share.** Include a card listing the ingredients. Several of our families have children with nut allergies, so please be careful about nuts and nut oils, and let us know if your dish contains them.

**Sign up on the sheet in the narthex** so we can plan the menu and avoid another round of nine pans of green beans.

We look forward to seeing you there. Bring your family, bring your neighbors, and bring a dish!
