**Harvest Potluck: Sunday, October 26**

Please join us after the 10:30 Mass on Sunday, October 26, in the parish hall for our annual Harvest Potluck. Last year 140 of you came, and we ran out of plates by noon, so this year we're asking everyone to plan ahead.

**Bring a dish to share.** Please include a card listing the ingredients. Several of our families have children with nut allergies, and the cards help everyone eat safely.

**Sign up in the narthex.** A sheet is posted there so we can coordinate who is bringing what. Please check it before you choose a dish, so we don't end up with nine pans of green beans again.

**Father Tom will bless the food** before we begin.

**The Knights of Columbus are smoking two briskets** for the meal, so come hungry.

We look forward to seeing you there. Bring your family, bring your appetite, and bring a dish if you can.
