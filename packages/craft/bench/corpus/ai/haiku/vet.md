**Subject: New Saturday Hours and Other Updates at Ruiz Animal Clinic**

Dear Ruiz Animal Clinic Clients,

Starting March 1, we will be open on Saturdays from 8:00 a.m. to noon. Saturday visits are for sick pets and vaccines only. We will not perform surgeries on Saturdays, so please schedule surgical procedures on a weekday.

On March 3, Dr. Tom Pak joins our team. He comes to us from Pullman, and we are glad to welcome him to Yakima. Wellness visits will remain Monday through Friday.

Walk-ins are welcome and seen on a first-come, first-served basis. If your pet is vomiting or limping, please call ahead so we can prepare for your visit.

Beginning now, we close for lunch from 12:00 to 1:00 p.m. on weekdays. Please plan appointments and drop-offs around that time.

Our rabies clinic is Sunday, March 15. The cost is $18 per shot, and there is no exam fee.

For after-hours emergencies, call our emergency line at 509-555-0142.

Thank you for trusting us with your pets' care. We look forward to seeing you and your animals.

Warmly,

Dr. Ana Ruiz
Ruiz Animal Clinic
Yakima, Washington
