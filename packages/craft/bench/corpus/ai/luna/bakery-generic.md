# Why We Switched to a Sourdough Starter

We switched to a sourdough starter for a simple reason: we wanted our bread to taste more like bread.

A starter is a living culture of flour and water. Given time, it helps dough rise and brings a depth of flavor that’s hard to rush. Our loaves now have a gentle tang, a crisp, burnished crust, and a tender crumb shaped by the slow rhythm of fermentation.

That slower process changes more than the taste. Working with a starter asks us to pay attention: to the dough’s feel, the room’s temperature, and the moment a loaf is ready for the oven. Each batch has its own pace, and learning to follow it has made us more thoughtful bakers.

We also love that a starter connects us to generations of bread makers. Ours is something we feed, care for, and bring back to the bench each day. It gives every bake a shared beginning, even as the weather and flour make each one a little different.

Switching took patience, but the first warm loaf made the reason clear. We’re glad to make bread this way, and grateful to share it with you.
