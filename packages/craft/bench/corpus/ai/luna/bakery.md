# Our Country Loaf, Back on the Counter

In January 2025, we made a big change at Kessler’s Bread on Main Street in Sidney: we switched from commercial yeast to a sourdough starter.

The starter came from my grandmother’s 1962 crock. I knew its history, but I didn’t know how much it would change our days at the bakery. A loaf that once took about four hours now took 20. For the first four months, we lost money as we learned to work with the slower rhythm.

We also had to make some hard choices. We used to bake 11 kinds of bread. We kept three.

The country loaf was one of them. By September, we were selling 140 a week, up from 60. That kind of response means a lot to a small bakery, and I’m grateful to everyone who gave the new loaf a try.

One customer, Mr. Albright, told me he feels his blood sugar handles our sourdough better. That’s his personal experience, and it isn’t a medical claim or a promise about how sourdough will affect anyone else. I’m glad he shared it with me, and I’m glad he enjoys the bread.

If you’re curious, come by Saturday morning and try a slice of our country loaf. I’ll be happy to see you.

—Ruth Kessler, Kessler’s Bread, Main Street, Sidney, Ohio
