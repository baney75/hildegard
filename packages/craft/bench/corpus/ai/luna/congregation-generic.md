Subject: Repairing Our Church Roof

Dear Congregation,

Our church roof needs repairs to protect the building and the people who gather here. We are arranging the work and will share the schedule as soon as it is confirmed.

We will pay for the repairs through a dedicated roof fund, using available church funds and contributions from members and friends. We will keep the congregation informed about the costs, funds raised, and any remaining balance.

If you would like to contribute, please mark your gift “Roof Repair.” Every contribution will help us care for our church home.

Thank you for your generosity and for helping preserve this place of worship for our congregation and future generations.

With gratitude,  
[Church Leadership]
