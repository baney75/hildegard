Dear Grace Lutheran Family,

The sanctuary roof is leaking over the north pews, and we need to repair it. Hauge Roofing has submitted a bid of $64,200. We have $21,500 in the building fund, leaving $42,700 to raise.

At its recent meeting, the church council voted 7–1 to ask the congregation to help raise the remaining amount by Easter. We’ll gather to discuss the roof project at the congregation meeting on Sunday, February 16, after worship.

There are already two ways to contribute. Our pancake breakfast on March 8 will direct all proceeds to the roof. And Evelyn Marsh has offered a $5,000 matching gift if we receive $20,000 in other gifts by March 31.

Thank you for caring for our church home and for one another. I hope you’ll join us after worship on February 16 as we consider how to move forward together.

In Christ,

Pastor Greg Hollis  
Grace Lutheran Church  
Ames, Iowa
