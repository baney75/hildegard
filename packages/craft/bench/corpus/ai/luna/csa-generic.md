**This Week at the Farm**

The fields are settling into the rhythm of the season, and we’re grateful to share another week’s harvest with you. Thank you for showing up for local food and for the people who grow it.

**In your share**

Look for [this week’s produce] in your box. Store leafy greens in the fridge in a loosely closed bag, and keep root vegetables cool and dry. If anything looks unfamiliar, try it simply: a little olive oil, salt, and a hot pan can bring out the best in almost any fresh vegetable.

**A kitchen idea**

Turn your greens into a quick pesto: blend them with garlic, a handful of nuts or seeds, grated cheese if you like, olive oil, and a squeeze of lemon. Toss with pasta, spread on toast, or thin with a little water for a bright dressing.

**Around the farm**

Every share helps keep our fields growing and our farm community connected. We appreciate the care you bring to picking up your box, returning reusable containers, and making room for seasonal surprises in your kitchen.

Enjoy the harvest, and we’ll see you at pickup!
