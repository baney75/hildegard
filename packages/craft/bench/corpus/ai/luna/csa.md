# Week 9 at Reyes Family Farm

Hello, everyone—

We’re at week 9 of our 20-week season. This week’s boxes have tomatoes, green beans, two zucchini, a head of cabbage, and our first Walla Walla onions of the season. Marisol tucked a recipe card for green bean salad into each box. We hope you enjoy it.

A tough update from the potato field: late blight hit, and we lost about a third of the crop. We won’t have potatoes for your boxes again until week 13. Thanks for bearing with us while the remaining plants recover.

Tuesday’s windstorm brought down the walnut tree. It missed the greenhouse by just six feet—a close call, and a reminder to appreciate a quiet day on the farm.

Pickup is Thursday from 3 to 6 p.m. at the barn. Please bring back your box and any egg cartons.

See you Thursday,

Tom Reyes  
Reyes Family Farm  
Outside Corvallis, Oregon
