# About Petal & Pine

Petal & Pine began in 2012, when I, Nadine Fournier, opened a flower shop in a former hardware store on Center Street in Rutland. These days, the old shop’s shelves make room for buckets of fresh flowers and the tools of the trade.

Our small team includes two full-time designers, Josh and Leah, and three part-time florists who join us during the holidays. We grow dahlias and zinnias on two acres in Pittsford, and source the rest of our flowers through a co-op in Brattleboro.

We design for all kinds of days, including weddings and funerals. Couples typically book their wedding flowers nine months ahead. Last year, we made 1,400 funeral arrangements, each with care for the people and memories they represent.

We work with fresh flowers only; I don’t make artificial arrangements. We deliver within 15 miles of the shop Tuesday through Saturday for $12.
