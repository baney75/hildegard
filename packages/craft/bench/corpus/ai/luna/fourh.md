# Clover Creek Champions  
## November Newsletter

The Clover Creek Champions are growing! We have 23 members this year, up from 17 last year. Welcome to everyone who has joined us.

At the Marshall County Fair in July, Hannah Yoder’s Hampshire gilt won reserve grand champion. Congratulations, Hannah!

Our fall pecan fundraiser brought in 410 pounds sold and cleared $1,180. That will pay for our spring trip to the state fairgrounds. We’re looking for two parent volunteers to drive on the trip. Please let the club know if you can help.

**December meeting:** Tuesday, December 9, at 7 p.m.  
**Location:** Bremen Public Library

The fire station hall is being repainted, so we’ll meet at the library instead. Hope to see you there!
