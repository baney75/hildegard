# What to Bring on a Fall Steelhead Float Trip

Fall steelhead trips bring cool mornings, changing weather, and fish that can test your patience and tackle. A little preparation makes it easier to stay comfortable and ready from the first drift to the last. Here’s what to pack for a day on the river.

## Dress for changing weather

Layering is the best way to handle a chilly start and a warmer afternoon. Wear a moisture-wicking base layer, a warm midlayer, and a waterproof outer layer. A rain jacket and rain pants help keep you comfortable if the weather turns or spray comes over the boat.

Dress for the water, too. Wear warm socks and properly fitted waders with a wading belt. Pack a dry set of clothes in a waterproof bag and leave it in the vehicle for after the trip.

## Protect your hands and feet

Cold fingers can make it tough to tie knots or handle gear. Bring warm gloves that still let you use your hands, plus a spare pair in case the first gets wet. A warm hat and a neck gaiter are useful when the wind picks up, and polarized sunglasses help reduce glare while protecting your eyes.

## Bring the essentials

Keep your personal gear simple and secure:

- Fishing license and any required tags
- Phone in a waterproof case
- Water and snacks
- Sunscreen, even on overcast days
- Any personal medication
- Small towel and a dry bag for valuables

A thermos of something warm can be a welcome addition on a cold morning.

## Use tackle suited to the river

The right rod, line, and presentation depend on the river and conditions. Before the trip, check with your guide about what to bring and whether fishing gear is provided. If you’re bringing your own, ask about the setup, terminal tackle, and bait or lures allowed on that water. Regulations can vary by river and season, so make sure your tackle and methods comply with current rules.

## Pack light and listen to your guide

Space is limited in a drift boat, and loose gear can get in the way. Bring only what you’ll use, and keep it in a small pack or secure bag. Your guide can help with the day’s plan, current conditions, and any last-minute adjustments.

A few thoughtful choices go a long way: stay warm, keep essentials dry, and arrive ready to enjoy the river.
