# What to Bring for a Fall Steelhead Float Trip on the Pere Marquette River

A fall steelhead float on the Pere Marquette means crisp mornings, cold water and long stretches on the river. Trips run from October through early December, when water temperatures are typically 38 to 46 degrees. Dress for warmth and comfort, and you’ll be ready to enjoy the day.

## Dress in warm, dry layers

Wear layers you can add or remove as the day changes. Choose wool or synthetic fabrics, and leave cotton at home: it holds moisture and can leave you cold.

Bring breathable waders and wading boots **without felt soles**. Michigan bans felt on some rivers, and felt-soled boots are not allowed in my boat.

A warm hat, gloves and a waterproof jacket are good additions for a chilly morning on the water.

## Bring your fishing license

Each angler needs a valid Michigan fishing license with an all-species endorsement. Be sure to have it with you for the trip.

## Leave the fishing gear to me

I supply the rods, reels, flies and drift boat. You can focus on dressing for the weather and getting ready to fish.

## Trip details

We launch at 7 a.m. from the Gleason’s Landing access. The trip is $525 for two people.

Pack your layers, bring your license and meet me at the river for a fall steelhead float on the Pere Marquette.
