## Our Story

Becker Hardware began in 1955 with my grandfather Walt, a $2,000 loan and a simple idea: give Hastings a hardware store where people could find what they need and get a little help along the way.

My dad added the garden center in 1982. Since then, we’ve kept building on what our family started. We sell paint and plumbing supplies, weigh fasteners by the pound, cut keys and glass, and help with the jobs that bring people through our doors.

In 2019, four feet of floodwater filled our basement. Before long, neighbors showed up with shop vacs to help us get back on our feet. We still remember that kindness. It says a lot about the town we’re proud to serve.

Today, Becker Hardware has 11 employees. Doris has worked the register for 31 years, and she’s the longest-serving member of our crew. We’re open Monday through Saturday, 7:30 a.m. to 6 p.m.

We’re not trying to beat the big box stores on price. We’re here to offer useful advice, a well-stocked store and the kind of service that comes from knowing our neighbors.

—Ray Becker
