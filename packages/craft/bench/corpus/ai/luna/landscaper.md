# Fall Lawn Care in Duluth: Get Your Grass Ready for Winter

**By Janet Moss, Moss Lawn and Garden**

Fall is the best time to help your lawn recover from summer and prepare for Duluth’s long winter. A few well-timed steps can make a real difference come spring.

## Aerate and overseed by September 20

Plan to aerate and overseed between August 20 and September 20, while the soil is still warm enough for new grass seed to take root. Aeration loosens compacted soil and helps water, air, and seed reach the roots.

We charge $140 to aerate a quarter acre. If your lawn feels hard underfoot or has thin, bare patches, fall is a good time to give it some attention.

## Keep leaves from covering the grass

Rake leaves or mulch them with your mower throughout fall. A thick layer left on the lawn can block light and hold moisture against the grass.

Last year, I saw a customer on Lakeside Drive lose half a lawn to snow mold after leaves were left on all winter. Don’t let a blanket of leaves turn into a springtime headache.

## Mow the last time at 2.5 inches

Duluth’s first hard frost usually arrives around October 5. Keep mowing as needed until the grass stops growing, then make your final cut at 2.5 inches. Cutting shorter can stress the lawn; leaving it too long can make it more vulnerable to winter problems.

## Stop fertilizing after October 15

Avoid fertilizing after October 15. As the season winds down, your lawn needs time to prepare for winter, not a late push of new growth.

A little fall maintenance now can help your lawn emerge healthier next spring. Keep up with leaves, finish mowing at the right height, and take advantage of the late-summer window for aeration and overseeding.
