**Maple Grove Little League Spring Newsletter**  
*Spring 2026*

Hello, Maple Grove families!

Spring is almost here, and we’re looking forward to another great season on the fields. We have **14 teams this year, up from 11**—a wonderful sign of how much our league is growing.

**Registration closes March 12.** The fee is $95 per child, with a reduced fee of $60 for the second sibling. Please register before the deadline so we can get everyone ready for the season.

Coaches, join us for the **coaches clinic on March 22 at the high school gym**. It’s a chance to prepare for the season and help make it a fun, positive experience for every player.

We also need a hand getting Veterans Field ready. Our **Saturday work day is April 5 at 9 a.m.**, and we’re looking for **20 parents with rakes and drills**. If you can pitch in, your help will make a real difference.

**Opening Day is April 26!** We’ll kick off with a parade from the fire station at 9 a.m. Come celebrate our players and the start of a new season.

One tough winter brought an unexpected repair: the January ice storm damaged the snack shack roof. Repairs will cost $11,000. Insurance covered $7,800, and the Kiwanis Club generously contributed $1,500. We’re grateful for that support as we work to cover the remaining cost and keep the snack shack ready for the season.

Thank you to the families, coaches, volunteers, and community partners who make Maple Grove Little League possible. I can’t wait to see everyone at the field.

Play ball!

**Gary Tolliver**  
President, Maple Grove Little League
