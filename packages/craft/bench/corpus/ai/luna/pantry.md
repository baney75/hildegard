**Cedar Falls Community Pantry Update**

October was a busy month at the Cedar Falls Community Pantry. We served 412 families, up from 331 in October last year, and welcomed 61 families who were visiting us for the first time.

We’re grateful to Hy-Vee for donating 1,800 pounds of produce. Right now, we especially need peanut butter, canned chicken, and diapers in sizes 4 and 5.

The pantry is open Tuesdays from 4 to 7 p.m. and Saturdays from 9 a.m. to noon at 218 West 3rd Street. We’re also looking for two more volunteer drivers to help with Friday pickups.

Mark your calendar: the Lions Club food drive is November 22. Thank you for supporting our neighbors.

—Barb Lindqvist, Volunteer Coordinator
