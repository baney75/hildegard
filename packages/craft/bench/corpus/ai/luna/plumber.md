# About Dobbs Plumbing and Drain

I’m Carl Dobbs. After eleven years as a journeyman plumber with a large outfit, I started Dobbs Plumbing and Drain in Waco in 2004. Today, it’s me and two other plumbers, Luis and Danny, with my wife, Pat, answering the phone.

We handle plumbing repairs, water heaters and drain cleaning. We don’t do new construction. Before I start a job, I’ll give you a flat-rate price so you know what the work will cost. And if a pipe bursts, we’re on call any hour.

Winter keeps us busy with water heaters. I fix about six a week that time of year. Whatever the job, I’ll tell you what I think needs fixing—and I won’t sell you a repipe you don’t need.
