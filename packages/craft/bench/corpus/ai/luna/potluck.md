Join us for St. Brendan’s Harvest Potluck on Sunday, October 26, after the 10:30 Mass in the parish hall. Father Tom will bless the food, and the Knights of Columbus will smoke two briskets.

Bring a dish to share, with an ingredient card—especially important for our two children with nut allergies. Please sign up on the sheet in the narthex so we can plan a good variety. Last year, 140 people joined us, and we ran out of plates by noon, so come hungry and arrive early!
