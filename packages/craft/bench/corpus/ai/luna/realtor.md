## About Teresa Walsh

Buying or selling a home is a big step. I’m Teresa Walsh, a real estate agent with Lakeshore Realty in Ludington, Michigan, and I’m here to make the process clear, thoughtful, and a little less daunting.

I grew up in Scottville, just nine miles east of Ludington, and have been licensed since 2011. Before real estate, I taught fourth grade for six years—a job that taught me to listen closely, explain things clearly, and meet people where they are.

I’ve helped clients buy and sell 312 homes, with about half of those sales going to first-time buyers. Whether you’re purchasing your first home or preparing for your next chapter, I’ll help you understand each step and make decisions with confidence. I also speak Spanish.

My work is focused on Mason and Lake counties, the communities I know and serve. You can reach me by text until 9 p.m. at (231) 555-0144.
