Subject: New Saturday Hours at Ruiz Animal Clinic

Dear Clients,

Starting March 1, Ruiz Animal Clinic will be open Saturdays from 8 a.m. to noon for sick visits and vaccines. We will not perform surgeries on Saturdays. Walk-ins are seen on a first-come, first-served basis. Please call ahead if your pet is vomiting or limping.

Wellness visits remain available Monday through Friday. On weekdays, we’ll now close for lunch from noon to 1 p.m.

Please join us in welcoming Dr. Tom Pak, who joins our team from Pullman on March 3.

Our rabies clinic is March 15. Rabies vaccines are $18 per shot, with no exam fee.

For after-hours emergencies, call 509-555-0142.

We look forward to seeing you and your pets!

Dr. Ana Ruiz  
Ruiz Animal Clinic  
Yakima, Washington
