**Subject: Our Sanctuary Roof, and How We Can Fix It Together**

Dear Grace Lutheran family,

Many of you have noticed the buckets by the north pews on rainy Sundays. The sanctuary roof is leaking, and it needs to be repaired before the damage spreads to the ceiling, walls, and the pews where so many of us have worshiped for years. I want to share where things stand and how you can help.

**Where we are**

We received a bid from Hauge Roofing for $64,200 to repair the sanctuary roof. Thanks to years of faithful giving, our building fund holds $21,500. That leaves **$42,700** still to raise.

The church council has discussed this carefully. By a vote of 7 to 1, the council decided to ask the congregation to help us raise the remaining amount by Easter. I'm grateful for the council's thoughtful work, including the honest questions raised along the way. Good stewardship means weighing decisions like this with care.

**A matching gift**

Evelyn Marsh has generously offered a **$5,000 matching gift** if we reach $20,000 in other gifts by March 31. Thank you, Evelyn. If we meet that goal, your gift will go even further. Please consider giving early so we can meet the match.

**Ways to take part**

- **Join us for the congregation meeting on Sunday, February 16, right after worship.** We'll go over the bid, the timeline, and the plan, and there will be plenty of time for your questions.
- **Come to the pancake breakfast on March 8.** Every dollar raised goes to the roof. Bring your family, bring your neighbors, and bring your appetite.
- **Give as you are able.** Gifts of every size matter. Please mark your offering "Roof Fund" so it is directed properly.

This building is more than a roof and walls. It is where we baptize our children, gather at the Lord's table, and say goodbye to those we love. Caring for it is one way we care for one another and for those who will worship here after us.

Thank you for your prayers, your generosity, and your faithfulness. I look forward to seeing you on February 16.

In Christ,

Pastor Greg Hollis
Grace Lutheran Church
Ames, Iowa
