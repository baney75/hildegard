Subject: Our Sanctuary Roof: Where We Stand and How You Can Help

Dear members and friends of Grace Lutheran,

Many of you have noticed the water stains, and some of you have moved from your usual seats on rainy Sundays. The sanctuary roof is leaking over the north pews, and it is time for us to repair it together.

**What the repair will cost**

The church council sought a bid for the work, and Hauge Roofing has quoted $64,200. Thanks to years of faithful giving, our building fund holds $21,500. That leaves **$42,700** still to raise.

The council voted 7 to 1 to bring this need to the whole congregation and ask for your help raising the remaining amount by Easter.

**A matching gift**

Evelyn Marsh has generously offered a $5,000 matching gift. If we reach $20,000 in other gifts by **March 31**, the match takes effect and brings us $5,000 closer to our goal. Every gift you give before that date helps unlock the match.

**Pancake breakfast, March 8**

Come hungry on **March 8** for a pancake breakfast. All proceeds will go to the roof. It is a good way to give, share a meal, and bring a neighbor along.

**Congregation meeting, Sunday, February 16**

Please stay after worship on **Sunday, February 16** for a congregation meeting. We will go over the bid, the timeline, and the plan for raising the funds, and there will be time for your questions.

The psalmist writes, "Unless the Lord builds the house, those who build it labor in vain." This building is a gift that has sheltered generations of worship, baptisms, weddings, and funerals. Caring for it is one way we give thanks for that gift and prepare to welcome those who come after us.

Thank you for your prayers, your generosity, and your faithfulness. I look forward to seeing you on February 16.

In Christ,

Pastor Greg Hollis
Grace Lutheran Church
Ames, Iowa
