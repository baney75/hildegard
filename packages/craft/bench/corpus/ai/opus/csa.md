Marisol has tucked a **recipe card for her green bean salad** into every box. It's the one we make at home when the beans come in faster than we can keep up with. Give it a try and let her know what you think.

## The Potato Report

I wish I had better news on this one. Late blight got into the potatoes, and we lost about a third of the crop. Blight moves fast once it takes hold, and we pulled what we could save as quickly as we could. The good news is that the rest of the potatoes are sound and curing well.

To give them the time they need, **there won't be potatoes in the boxes until week 13.** We'll make up for it with what's coming on strong in the meantime. Thank you for your patience. This is part of what it means to share a season with a small farm, and we're grateful you're in it with us.

## The Walnut Tree

If you've driven by since Tuesday, you may have noticed the view looks a little different. The windstorm brought down the big walnut tree by the greenhouse. It came down hard and landed **six feet from the greenhouse**. Six feet. We spent a good while just standing there looking at it.

Nobody was hurt, the greenhouse is fine, and everything growing inside it is fine. We'll miss that old tree and the shade it gave, but we're counting ourselves lucky. It'll be firewood and a good story for years to come.
