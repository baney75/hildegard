Hello, everyone,

We're officially almost halfway through the season. Thanks for sticking with us through what's turned out to be an eventful week.

## What's in Your Box

- **Tomatoes**
- **Green beans**
- **Two zucchini**
- **A head of cabbage**
- **The first Walla Walla onions of the year**

The Walla Wallas are the ones I look forward to most. They're sweet enough to slice raw onto a sandwich or into a salad, and they don't keep as long as storage onions, so enjoy them sooner rather than later.

## A Recipe from Marisol

You'll find a recipe card from Marisol tucked into each box for a green bean salad. With beans, tomatoes, and those new onions all in the same box, you've got most of what you need right there.

## Potato News (The Not-So-Good Kind)

Late blight got into the potato field, and we lost about a third of the crop. Blight moves fast once it takes hold, and there's not much to do but pull the damaged plants and protect what's left. The good news is that two-thirds of the planting came through. **There won't be potatoes in the boxes until week 13**, but they will be back. I'm sorry to make you wait, and I appreciate your patience. This is part of what sharing in a farm's season means, the lean weeks along with the full ones.

## The Walnut Tree

The windstorm on Tuesday brought down the old walnut tree. It fell **six feet from the greenhouse**. I've been walking past that tree my whole life, and I'll miss it, but I'm grateful it landed where it did. Everything growing in the greenhouse is safe, and so is everyone here. We'll be cleaning up and cutting wood for a while.
