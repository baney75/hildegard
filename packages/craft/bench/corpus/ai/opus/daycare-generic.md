Dear Families,

Thank you for trusting us with your children each day. Watching them grow, play, and learn together is a joy, and keeping them healthy is one of our most important jobs. With cold and flu season here, we're updating our illness policy. **The changes take effect Monday, December 7, 2026.**

**What's Changing**

Children must now be **fever-free for a full 24 hours without fever-reducing medication** (such as Tylenol or Motrin) before returning to care. Our previous policy allowed a return after 12 hours.

The same 24-hour rule now applies to vomiting and diarrhea. Children should be symptom-free for 24 hours before coming back.

**Why We're Making This Change**

In a small home setting, our children share toys, meals, nap space, and lots of hugs. When one child is contagious, illness spreads quickly to the other children, their siblings, and their parents. The 24-hour guideline is recommended by pediatricians and public health agencies because many children are still contagious after a fever first breaks. We also know that fever reducers can hide a fever for several hours, which is why we ask that children be fever-free without them.

This change also helps us stay open. When a caregiver gets sick, every family is affected, and we want to avoid unexpected closures as much as we can.

**Please Keep Your Child Home If They Have:**

- A temperature of 100.4°F or higher
- Vomiting or diarrhea
- A rash with fever, or a rash that hasn't been checked by a doctor
- Pink eye with discharge, until 24 hours after starting treatment
- Signs of being too unwell to join normal activities, such as unusual tiredness, constant crying, or trouble breathing

**If Your Child Becomes Sick During the Day**

We will call you and ask that your child be picked up **within one hour**. Until you arrive, your child will rest comfortably and apart from the other children. Please make sure your emergency contact information is current, and consider arranging a backup person who can pick up on short notice.

**We Understand**

We know how hard it is to miss work or rearrange your day when your little one is sick. These decisions are never meant to make life harder for you. They are meant to protect every child in our care, including yours. Thank you for your patience and partnership.

If you have any questions or would like to talk through how this might affect your family, please reach out at drop-off, at pickup, or by phone. We're always happy to chat.

With gratitude and warm wishes for a healthy season,

Your Child Care Team
