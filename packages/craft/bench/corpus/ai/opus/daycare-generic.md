Dear Families,

I hope this note finds you well and enjoying the crisp fall weather. As we head into the colder months, I want to share an important update to our illness policy that will take effect on **Monday, December 1, 2026**.

**What's changing**

Beginning December 1, children who have had a fever of 100.4°F or higher must be **fever-free for a full 24 hours without the use of fever-reducing medication** (such as Tylenol or Motrin) before returning to care. Previously, our policy allowed children to return the morning after a fever broke. The same 24-hour rule will now also apply to vomiting and diarrhea.

If your child develops a fever, vomiting, or diarrhea while in my care, I will call you and ask that your child be picked up within one hour. Until you arrive, I will keep your child comfortable and resting in a quiet space away from the other children.

**Why we're making this change**

Last winter, we saw several illnesses pass from child to child in quick succession, and a few of you missed more work than any of us would have liked. Many of these illnesses are still contagious even after a fever first breaks, and medication can mask symptoms without stopping the spread. The 24-hour guideline is recommended by pediatricians and public health agencies, and it's the standard most larger centers already follow. Because our home daycare is a small group in shared space, one sick child can affect everyone quickly. This change is about protecting all of our little ones, and your families too.

**What this means for you**

I know that keeping a child home for an extra day can be a real challenge, especially for working parents. I'd gently encourage every family to think now about a backup care plan, whether that's a grandparent, a friend, a neighbor, or a flexible work arrangement, so that you aren't scrambling when the sniffles hit. Please also make sure your emergency contact information is up to date. I'll be sending home a short form next week for you to review and return.

As always, tuition remains the same for sick days, as your child's spot is held for them.

**What hasn't changed**

Children with mild colds, such as a runny nose or a light cough, are still welcome as long as they feel well enough to play and participate in our normal day. If you're ever unsure whether your child should come in, please call or text me before drop-off and we'll figure it out together.

I'm so grateful for each of your families and for the trust you place in me every day. Your children bring so much joy to our home, and keeping them healthy and happy is my top priority. If you have any questions or concerns about this change, please don't hesitate to reach out. I'm always happy to talk.

Warmly,

Your Child Care Provider
