Dear Little Acorns Families,

Thank you for trusting me with your children. Over the past six years, Little Acorns has cared for 31 families, and watching your little ones grow, play, and learn together has been the joy of my working life. I'm writing to share a few updates to our policies, along with some important dates for the year ahead.

**Weekly Rate**

Starting June 1, the weekly rate will increase from $265 to $285. I don't make this change lightly. Keeping our program safe and well-run means ongoing investment in the home and yard. Most recently, we replaced the fence around the play area at a cost of $3,200. This adjustment helps me keep up with those costs while continuing to provide the same attentive care your children are used to. Our small size hasn't changed: I care for no more than 8 children, ages 1 to 4, together with my assistant, Priya.

**Late Pickup**

Pickup is by 5:30 p.m. After 5:30, a late fee of $1 per minute will apply. Priya and I have families of our own waiting for us at the end of the day, and I appreciate your help in keeping evenings on schedule. If you know you're running behind, please call or text me as soon as you can.

**Sunscreen Policy**

With sunny Central Oregon days ahead, we'll be spending lots of time outdoors. Under our new sunscreen policy, every family must sign a sunscreen permission form before I can apply sunscreen to your child. I'll send the form home soon. Please return it signed so your child can enjoy outdoor play safely.

**Closures**

Please mark your calendars. Little Acorns will be closed:

- The week of July 4
- Thanksgiving Thursday and Friday

**Open House**

I'd love to see you! I'll be hosting an open house on **May 10 from 10:00 a.m. to noon**. Come see the new fence, say hello to Priya, ask questions about these changes, or simply visit the space where your child spends their days. Friends and neighbors who might be looking for care are welcome too.

As always, my door is open. If you have any questions or concerns about these updates, please talk with me at drop-off or pickup, or reach out anytime.

Thank you for being part of the Little Acorns family.

Warmly,

Marcy Ellison
Little Acorns Home Daycare
Bend, Oregon
