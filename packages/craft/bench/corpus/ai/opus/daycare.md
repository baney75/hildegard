Dear Little Acorns Families,

Thank you for being part of our daycare family. Over the past six years, Little Acorns has cared for 31 families, and it has been a joy to watch so many little ones grow, learn, and play together here in Bend. I'm writing to share some updates to our policies and schedule. Please read through, and don't hesitate to reach out with any questions.

**Weekly Rate Change**
Starting June 1, our weekly rate will increase from $265 to $285. I don't take this change lightly. Rising costs for food, supplies, and upkeep make it necessary, and it allows me to keep providing the safe, loving, and well-staffed environment your children deserve. As you may have noticed, we recently replaced the fence around our play yard at a cost of $3,200. Investments like this keep our outdoor space secure for everyone.

**Late Pickup**
Our day ends at 5:30 p.m. Beginning with this policy update, pickups after 5:30 will be charged $1 per minute. Priya and I have families of our own waiting at home, and timely pickups help us end each day well. If you're running late, please call or text so we know your child's plans.

**Sunscreen Policy**
With sunny days ahead, we're spending lots of time outside. Our new sunscreen policy requires a signed permission form for each child before we can apply sunscreen. Forms will be available at pickup, and I'll ask that they be returned as soon as possible so no one misses outdoor play.

**Closures**
Please mark your calendars. Little Acorns will be closed:
- The week of July 4
- Thanksgiving Thursday and Friday

**Our Program**
As a reminder, we care for up to 8 children, ages 1 to 4. My wonderful assistant, Priya, and I work together to give every child individual attention in a small, home-based setting.

**Open House**
I'll be hosting an open house on May 10 from 10 a.m. to noon. It's a great chance to visit with Priya and me, see our new fence and play yard, ask questions about these updates, and pick up your sunscreen form. Please feel free to tell friends and neighbors who might be looking for care.

Thank you for trusting me with your children every day. It's truly a privilege, and I look forward to many more happy days together.

Warmly,

Marcy Ellison
Little Acorns Home Daycare
Bend, Oregon
