# About Petal & Pine

In 2012, I signed a lease on an old hardware store on Center Street in Rutland. The shelves that once held nails and paint cans now hold buckets of stems, and on a good morning the whole place smells like a garden. That's Petal & Pine.

## Where our flowers come from

We grow our own dahlias and zinnias on two acres in Pittsford, just up the road. From midsummer until the first hard frost, many of the flowers in our shop were cut that week by people you'll meet at the counter. Everything else comes from a growers' co-op in Brattleboro, so even in the depths of a Vermont winter we're buying close to home and from people we know.

Every flower in our shop is real. I don't sell artificial flowers, and I never will. Real flowers open, change, and fade, and I think that's a big part of why they mean something.

## The people behind the counter

Josh and Leah are our two full-time designers, and their work fills our cases and goes out our door every day. Around the holidays, three part-time helpers join us to keep up with the wreaths, centerpieces, and last-minute orders. We're a small team, and we like it that way. When you call, you'll talk to someone who will actually be making your arrangement.

## Weddings

We love doing weddings, and we give each one our full attention. That's why we book about nine months out. If you're planning a wedding, reach out early so we can save your date and start talking about what you have in mind.

## Sympathy and funeral flowers

A large part of our work is helping families through a loss. Last year we made 1,400 funeral arrangements for families across the Rutland area. We take that trust seriously. Whether you need a standing spray for a service or a small arrangement sent to a grieving friend's home, we'll handle it with care and get it there on time.

## Delivery

We deliver within 15 miles of the shop, Tuesday through Saturday, for a flat $12. If you're not sure whether an address falls within our area, give us a call and we'll let you know.

## Come see us

Stop by Center Street to see what's fresh this week, or call us to place an order. After all these years, it's still the best part of my job when someone walks in and says, "I'm not sure what I want, but it's for someone I love." We'll figure it out together.

— Nadine Fournier, owner
