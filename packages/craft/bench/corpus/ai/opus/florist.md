# About Petal & Pine

In 2012, I signed a lease on an old hardware store on Center Street in downtown Rutland. The shelves that once held nails and paint cans now hold buckets of ranunculus and eucalyptus, and some days I swear the floorboards still smell faintly of sawdust. I'm Nadine Fournier, and Petal & Pine is the shop I always wanted to walk into.

## Grown close to home

Many of the flowers we use start their lives just up the road. We grow dahlias and zinnias on two acres in Pittsford, and through the summer and into fall those blooms go from our field to your arrangement in a matter of hours. What we don't grow ourselves, we buy from a growers' co-op in Brattleboro, so the rest of our flowers are still coming from people who know the Vermont seasons as well as we do.

One promise I've kept since the day we opened: I don't do artificial flowers. Everything that leaves this shop was alive and growing not long before it reached your hands.

## The people behind the work

Josh and Leah are our two full-time designers, and their work fills our coolers, our front windows, and a good many Rutland kitchen tables. When the holidays arrive and orders pile up, three part-time staff join us to keep everything moving. We're a small team, and we like it that way. When you call, you'll talk to someone who knows the flowers and will likely be the one arranging yours.

## Weddings

Wedding work is some of the most rewarding we do. Our calendar fills about nine months ahead, so if you're planning a wedding, reach out early. We'll sit down with you and build flowers that fit the day you have in mind.

## Sympathy and funeral flowers

Last year we made 1,400 funeral arrangements. That number means a great deal to us. Each one was for a family going through a hard week, and we take that trust seriously. If you've lost someone, call us. We'll help you choose something fitting and make sure it arrives where and when it needs to.

## Delivery

We deliver within 15 miles of the shop, Tuesday through Saturday, for a flat $12. Birthdays, anniversaries, apologies, or no reason at all, we'll get it there.

## Come see us

Stop by the shop on Center Street. Come in for a single stem or a full bouquet, or just to see what's fresh from the field this week. We'd love to meet you.

*— Nadine Fournier, owner*
