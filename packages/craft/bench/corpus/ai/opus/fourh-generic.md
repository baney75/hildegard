## Record Books Are Due

Your record book tells the story of your 4-H year. Please bring your completed record book to our next meeting so leaders can review it before it goes to the county office.

**A complete record book includes:**
- Project goals and what you learned
- Income and expense records
- Photos of your project work
- Leadership and community service activities
- Your personal 4-H story

If you're stuck, ask an older member or club leader for help. Starting early makes the job much easier. A well-kept record book also strengthens applications for awards, trips, and scholarships.

## Animal Care in Cold Weather

For members raising livestock and small animals, colder weather brings new responsibilities:

- Check water daily. Frozen water buckets are a common winter problem.
- Provide dry bedding and shelter from wind.
- Watch body condition, since many animals need extra feed in cold months.
- Keep barns ventilated to prevent respiratory illness.

A healthy animal in winter means a stronger project come spring.
