# Our Story

In 1955, my grandfather Walt Becker borrowed $2,000 and opened a hardware store in Hastings, Nebraska. He didn't have a business plan. He had a belief that people here would need someone who knew the difference between a lag bolt and a carriage bolt, and who would take the time to explain it.

Seventy years later, we're still here, and we still take the time.

## What We Do

We sell paint, and we'll help you figure out how much you need so you don't buy too much. We carry plumbing parts for the job you're doing on a Saturday morning, and for the one you didn't plan on doing at all. We still sell fasteners by the pound, so if you need six screws, you buy six screws. We cut keys. We cut glass. We've probably fixed the same storm window your father brought in thirty years ago.

In 1982, my dad added the garden center. He figured that if folks were coming in for a new hose nozzle, they might want some tomato plants too. He was right. Every spring since, the garden center has been one of the busiest corners of the store, and one of the best places in town to swap advice about what's growing and what isn't.

## The Flood

In 2019, the flood put four feet of water in our basement. We lost inventory, equipment, and a fair amount of sleep. What we didn't lose was the sense of what this place means to people.

Before we'd even figured out where to start, neighbors started showing up with shop vacs. Customers, friends, and people we'd only ever seen across the counter came down those stairs and worked alongside us. We didn't ask them to. They just came.

We've always said this store belongs to Hastings as much as it belongs to the Becker family. That week, Hastings proved it.

## The People

Today, Becker Hardware has 11 employees. Some have been with us a few years, and some have been with us a lot longer than that. Doris has worked our register for 31 years. If you've shopped here, you know her. If you haven't, you will, and she'll probably remember your name by your second visit.

Our staff knows the stock, and more importantly, they know how things work. Bring in the broken part. Describe the drip, the squeak, or the crack. We'll help you sort it out.

## What We're Not

We're not trying to beat the big box stores on price. We can't, and honestly, we're not trying to. What we offer is something different: people who know what they're talking about, the right part instead of a package of twelve, and a store where the person helping you will still be here next year.

If that sounds like the kind of hardware store you want in your town, we'd love to see you.
