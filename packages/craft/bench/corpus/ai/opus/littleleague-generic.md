# Spring Is Here: Play Ball!

## Welcome to the New Season

The days are getting longer, the grass is turning green, and somewhere a parent is digging last year's cleats out of the garage and discovering they're two sizes too small. That can only mean one thing: Little League season is back.

Whether this is your child's first year swinging a bat or their last year before moving up, we're glad to have you. Our league runs on families like yours, and this season is shaping up to be one of our best yet.

## What to Expect This Season

**Evaluations and Team Placement**
Player evaluations help our managers build balanced teams so every game is competitive and fun. Kids don't need to be nervous. Evaluations aren't tryouts, and every registered player will be placed on a team. Just bring a glove, water, and a good attitude.

**Practices**
Once teams are formed, managers will reach out directly with practice times and locations. Early practices focus on fundamentals: throwing, catching, fielding, and base running. Please make sure your player arrives a few minutes early and ready to go.

**Opening Day**
Opening Day is the highlight of our spring. Join us for the player parade, team introductions, the ceremonial first pitch, and a full slate of games. Bring the whole family, bring your lawn chairs, and bring your loudest cheers.

## Gear Checklist

Before the first practice, make sure your player has:

- A glove that fits (not one they'll "grow into")
- Baseball or softball pants
- Cleats in the right size
- An athletic cup for boys
- A batting helmet if they prefer their own (league helmets are available)
- A water bottle with their name on it

Uniforms and caps are provided by the league and will be handed out by your team manager.

## Safety First

The health and safety of our players comes before everything else. A few reminders:

- **Hydrate.** Spring afternoons can heat up fast. Send plenty of water.
- **Watch the weather.** If you hear thunder or see lightning, all players and spectators must leave the field immediately. Play resumes only after 30 minutes without lightning.
- **Report injuries.** Let your manager know about any injury, no matter how small, and keep them informed about allergies or medical conditions.
- **Concussion awareness.** Any player with a suspected head injury must sit out until cleared by a medical professional.

## Volunteers Make It Happen

Every game you watch is made possible by volunteers. We need help in nearly every area:

- **Team parents** to coordinate snacks, communication, and end-of-season celebrations
- **Umpires**, with training provided for adults and older youth
- **Concession stand workers** for a shift or two this season
- **Field crew** to help with lining, dragging, and keeping our fields in great shape
- **Scorekeepers** for game days

You don't need baseball experience, just a willingness to help. Even one shift makes a real difference. Talk to your team manager or stop by the concession stand to sign up.

## The Concession Stand

Our concession stand is more than a place to grab a hot dog. It's one of the league's biggest fundraisers. Every snack, drink, and sunflower seed packet you buy helps keep registration costs down, maintain our fields, and fund new equipment. So go ahead, treat yourself.

## A Word on Sportsmanship

Little League is about more than wins and losses. It's about teamwork, effort, and learning to handle both success and disappointment with grace.

We ask everyone in the stands to do their part:

- Cheer for good plays, on both teams.
- Leave the coaching to the coaches.
- Respect the umpires, many of whom are volunteers and some of whom are still kids themselves.
- Remember that the players are watching how the grown-ups behave.

A positive word from the bleachers can make a young player's whole season.

## Thank You to Our Sponsors

Our local business sponsors help us keep this league affordable and accessible for every family. Please show them your support, and when you visit, let them know you appreciate what they do for our kids.

## Stay Connected

Schedules, field updates, rainout notices, and announcements will be shared through the league website and your team manager. Please make sure your contact information is up to date so you never miss an update.

## See You at the Ballpark

From the first pitch of Opening Day to the final out of the season, we can't wait to see our players grow, improve, and make memories that last a lifetime. Thank you for being part of our Little League family.
