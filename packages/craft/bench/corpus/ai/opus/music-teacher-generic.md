**Preparing for the Big Day**
Over the coming weeks, each student will be polishing a piece chosen especially for them. Here's how you can help at home:

- **Encourage regular practice.** Short, focused sessions most days are far more effective than one long session the night before a lesson.
- **Be an audience.** Ask your child to "perform" their piece for the family. Playing for others, even in the living room, builds confidence.
- **Celebrate progress, not perfection.** Mistakes happen to every musician. What matters most is that students learn to keep going and enjoy sharing their music.

**What to Wear**
Students should dress in comfortable, recital-appropriate clothing, such as a nice shirt, dress, or slacks. Please choose shoes that allow easy use of the pedals; hard-soled shoes work better than sneakers or sandals.
