# Food Pantry Update: Neighbors Feeding Neighbors This Season

As the weather turns colder and the holidays approach, our community food pantry is entering its busiest time of year. Thank you to everyone who has given food, time, or money. Here's what's happening and how you can help.

## What We're Seeing

Demand rises every fall. Heating bills climb, holiday costs pile up, and many families who usually get by find themselves stretched. More households have been visiting the pantry in recent weeks, including many first-time guests. Everyone is welcome. No one needs to explain their situation to receive food.

## What We Need Most

Our shelves empty fastest of these items:

- **Proteins:** canned tuna, chicken, peanut butter, and dried or canned beans
- **Breakfast foods:** cereal, oatmeal, and shelf-stable milk
- **Complete meals:** soups, stews, chili, and pasta with sauce
- **Holiday staples:** stuffing mix, canned vegetables, cranberry sauce, gravy, and boxed potatoes
- **Personal care items:** toothpaste, soap, shampoo, diapers, and feminine hygiene products

Please check expiration dates before donating, and avoid glass jars when you can, since they break easily. Monetary donations go especially far, because the pantry can buy in bulk through regional food bank partners and stretch every dollar further than a grocery store purchase.

## Volunteers Wanted

We're looking for extra hands through the end of the year. Volunteers sort donations, stock shelves, greet guests, and help carry groceries to cars. Shifts are flexible, no experience is needed, and families, youth groups, and coworkers are encouraged to sign up together. It's a great way to spend a few hours and meet your neighbors.

## Holiday Distribution

We're planning a special holiday distribution so families can share a festive meal. If you'd like to sponsor a holiday box, donate a turkey or ham, or help pack and distribute, please get in touch with the pantry soon. The earlier we know, the better we can plan.

## Getting Help

If you or someone you know could use a little extra support, please stop by during open hours. Pantry hours and drop-off times are posted at the pantry entrance and on our community website. Shopping is confidential and dignified, and our volunteers are happy to help you find what you need.

## Thank You

Every can, every dollar, and every hour makes a difference. This pantry runs because neighbors show up for one another, and this season we're grateful, as always, to be part of a community that does.

*Questions, donations, or volunteer interest? Reach out to the food pantry team. We'd love to hear from you.*
