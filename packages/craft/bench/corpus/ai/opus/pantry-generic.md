# Food Pantry Update: Heading Into the Holiday Season

As the weather turns colder and the holidays approach, our community food pantry is entering its busiest time of year. Thank you to everyone who has given food, money, or time over the past few months. Your generosity has kept our shelves stocked and our doors open for neighbors who need a little extra help.

## What We're Seeing

Demand at the pantry has grown steadily this fall. Rising grocery prices and the end of seasonal work mean more families are stopping by, many of them for the first time. We want everyone to know that the pantry is here for anyone in our community who needs it. No one needs to explain their situation, and every visitor is welcome.

## What We Need Most

Our shelves are in good shape for some staples, but we're running low on others. If you're planning to donate, these items would make the biggest difference right now:

- **Protein:** canned tuna, chicken, beans, and peanut butter
- **Breakfast items:** cereal, oatmeal, and shelf-stable milk
- **Hearty meals:** canned soups, stews, and chili
- **Holiday staples:** stuffing mix, canned vegetables, cranberry sauce, and gravy
- **Personal care:** toothpaste, soap, shampoo, diapers, and feminine hygiene products

Personal care items are often overlooked, and food assistance programs don't cover them, so they're especially appreciated.

Please check expiration dates before donating, and we ask that you avoid glass containers when possible, since they break easily during sorting and storage.

## Monetary Donations

Cash donations go a long way. Through our partnerships, the pantry can often buy food at a fraction of retail cost, so every dollar you give stretches further than it would at the grocery store. Monetary gifts also let us buy fresh produce, dairy, and other perishables that can't be collected through food drives.

## Volunteers Welcome

The holiday season brings more donations, and more donations mean more sorting, stocking, and distribution. We're looking for volunteers to help with:

- Sorting and shelving incoming donations
- Packing holiday meal boxes
- Greeting and assisting visitors during distribution hours
- Picking up donations from local businesses and drop-off sites

No experience is needed, and even a couple of hours makes a real difference. Volunteering is also a great way for families, friends, coworkers, and youth groups to give back together.

## Holiday Meal Boxes

We plan to distribute holiday meal boxes again this year so families can share a festive meal at home. If you'd like to sponsor a box, contribute ingredients, or help with packing, please reach out to the pantry. Details on sign-ups and distribution will be shared soon.

## Thank You

The pantry runs on the kindness of this community. Whether you drop a few cans in a donation bin, organize a food drive at work, or spend a Saturday morning stocking shelves, you are helping make sure no one in our neighborhood goes hungry this season.

From all of us at the pantry: thank you, and we hope to see you soon.
