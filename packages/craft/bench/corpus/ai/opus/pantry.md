**Cedar Falls Community Pantry: October Update**

October was our busiest month in recent memory. We served 412 families, up from 331 last October, an increase of nearly 25 percent. Sixty-one of those households came to us for the first time. Behind every one of those numbers is a neighbor, and we're grateful to be here for each of them.

**A big thank-you to Hy-Vee**

Hy-Vee donated 1,800 pounds of fresh produce this month. That gift put fruits and vegetables on hundreds of tables, and it made a real difference as demand climbed. Thank you to everyone at Hy-Vee who made it happen.

**What we need most right now**

Our shelves are running low on a few essentials:

- Peanut butter
- Canned chicken
- Diapers, sizes 4 and 5

If you're shopping this week, please consider picking up an extra jar or package. These items go out the door almost as fast as they come in.

**Drivers wanted for Fridays**

We need two more volunteer drivers for our Friday pickups. If you have a reliable vehicle and a few hours on Friday, this is one of the most direct ways to keep food flowing to the pantry. Stop by during open hours to let us know you're interested.

**Mark your calendar: Lions Club Food Drive, November 22**

The Lions Club is holding a food drive on Saturday, November 22, to benefit the pantry. It comes at just the right time as we head into the holidays. Please bring items from our needs list above if you can. Peanut butter, canned chicken and diapers will be especially welcome.
