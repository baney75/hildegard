**Pantry Update: More Neighbors Than Ever, and How You Can Help**

*From Barb Lindqvist, Volunteer Coordinator, Cedar Falls Community Pantry*

October was our busiest month in a long time. We served 412 families, up from 331 in October of last year. That's 81 more households, an increase of about 24 percent. Sixty-one of those families came to us for the first time. To each of them: welcome. We're glad you found us, and we hope you'll come back whenever you need to.

**A big thank-you to Hy-Vee**

Hy-Vee donated 1,800 pounds of fresh produce this past month. Fresh fruits and vegetables are some of the hardest items for us to keep on our shelves, and this gift put healthy food on hundreds of tables. Thank you, Hy-Vee, for being such a generous partner.

**What we need most right now**

Our shelves are running low on a few essentials:

- **Peanut butter**
- **Canned chicken**
- **Diapers, sizes 4 and 5**

If you're heading to the store, please consider picking up one of these items. Donations can be dropped off during our open hours.

**Wanted: two Friday drivers**

We're looking for two more volunteer drivers to help with our Friday pickups. If you have a reliable vehicle and a few hours on Friday, you'd be making a real difference. Stop by during pantry hours to let us know you're interested.

**Mark your calendar: Lions Club Food Drive, November 22**

The Lions Club is holding a food drive on Saturday, November 22, to benefit the pantry. With the holidays coming and more families relying on us than ever, this drive couldn't come at a better time. Please give if you can, and remember that peanut butter, canned chicken and diapers are at the top of our list.

— Barb Lindqvist
