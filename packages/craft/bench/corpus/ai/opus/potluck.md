**Harvest Potluck: Sunday, October 26**

Join your St. Brendan's family for our annual Harvest Potluck on Sunday, October 26, in the parish hall immediately following the 10:30 Mass. Father Tom will bless the food before we eat.

Last year about 140 of us gathered around the tables, and we ran out of plates by noon! We're expecting another big crowd, so come hungry and bring friends.

**Bring a dish to share.** The Knights of Columbus are smoking two briskets for the main course, so we're counting on you for sides, salads, breads, and desserts.

**Please sign up on the sheet in the narthex** and note what you plan to bring. This helps us keep a good variety on the table. We'd love to avoid another year of nine pans of green beans!

**Label your dish.** Please place a card next to your dish listing its ingredients. Several children in our parish have nut allergies, and a simple card helps every family eat safely and with confidence.

All are welcome. We hope to see you there as we give thanks together for the blessings of the harvest season.
