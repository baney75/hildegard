**Harvest Potluck: Come Hungry, Bring a Dish!**

Join your St. Brendan's family for our annual Harvest Potluck on **Sunday, October 26, right after the 10:30 Mass** in the parish hall. Father Tom will bless the food before we eat, and the Knights of Columbus are smoking two briskets for the occasion.

**Bring a dish to share,** and please include a card listing its ingredients. Two of our young parishioners have nut allergies, and a simple card helps every family eat safely and with peace of mind.

**Please sign up on the sheet in the narthex** so we know what's coming. We love green beans, but nine pans of them was plenty for a lifetime. A quick glance at the list will help us end up with a good mix of mains, sides, salads, and desserts.

Last year 140 of us gathered around the tables, and we ran out of plates by noon! We're planning for an even bigger crowd this year, so bring your friends, your neighbors, and your appetite.

We hope to see you there as we give thanks for the harvest and for one another.
