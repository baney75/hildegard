# About Me

There's a moment I've watched happen dozens of times. A family pulls into a driveway, steps out of the car, and before anyone says a word about square footage or closing costs, someone turns toward the water and goes quiet. That's usually when I know we're in the right place.

I'm a local real estate agent, and this town is home. I know which roads flood in a wet spring, which coves stay calm when the wind picks up, and which neighborhoods catch the best sunsets off the lake. I know the difference between a cottage that just needs some love and one that needs a new foundation. This isn't knowledge you can pull from a listing service. It comes from living here, paying attention, and caring how things turn out for the people I work with.

## How I Work

Buying or selling a home is a big decision, and in a small lakeside town it's often a personal one too. Maybe you're selling the place where your kids learned to swim. Maybe you're finally buying the retreat you've been saving for, or moving here full-time after years of summer visits. I take that seriously.

When you work with me, you can expect:

- **Honest advice.** If a house isn't right for you, I'll say so. If a price is too high, I'll tell you before the market does.
- **Real local insight.** Shoreline rules, septic and well questions, seasonal access, rental potential, and the quirks that come with waterfront property. I'll help you understand what you're getting into.
- **Clear communication.** You'll never wonder where things stand. I return calls, answer texts, and explain each step in plain language.
- **Patience.** Some people find their home in a weekend. Others take a year. Both are fine with me.

## For Sellers

Your home has a story, and buyers here are looking for more than four walls. I'll help you present your property in a way that shows what makes it special, whether that's the dock, the view from the porch, or a short walk into town. I'll price it with care, market it to the right people, and negotiate firmly on your behalf.

## For Buyers

Whether you want a year-round home, a weekend getaway, or an investment property, I'll help you find something that fits your life and your budget. I'll show you the town the way locals see it, point out what the photos leave out, and make sure you feel confident before you sign anything.

## More Than a Transaction

In a town this size, I'll see my clients at the grocery store, on the public beach, and at the fall festival for years to come. That's exactly how I want it. My business is built on relationships and referrals, and I'd rather earn your trust than close a quick deal.

If you're thinking about buying or selling around the lake, or you're just curious what your home might be worth, I'd love to talk. No pressure and no sales pitch, just a conversation with someone who knows this place and wants to help you find your spot on the water.
