# About Donovan

I didn't get into real estate to sell houses. I got into it because I love this town and wanted to help people find their place in it.

Anyone can pull listings off a website. What I offer is everything the listing doesn't tell you. Which coves stay calm when the wind picks up. Which roads get plowed first after a storm. Which street throws the best Fourth of July party, and which one you'd rather be on if you like to be asleep by ten. That knowledge comes from living here: walking the shoreline in every season, getting coffee at the same counter every morning, and knowing my neighbors by name.

## How I Work

**I listen first.** Some people want a year-round home with room for the kids to grow. Some want a weekend cabin where the phone stays off. Some are ready to downsize after decades in a house full of memories. I'd rather spend an hour understanding what you need than an afternoon showing you places that don't fit.

**I tell you the truth.** If a property has a drainage problem, a tired septic system, or a dock that won't survive another winter, you'll hear it from me before you hear it from an inspector. If your asking price is too high, I'll show you why. Honesty can be uncomfortable for a minute. Surprises after closing are much worse.

**I know lake property.** Waterfront homes come with their own questions: shoreline regulations, dock permits, well and septic, flood zones, seasonal access, and what a beautiful view costs to insure. I'll help you understand all of it so you can buy or sell with confidence.

**I stay in touch.** You won't have to wonder where things stand. I return calls, I answer texts, and I'll keep you updated from first showing to final signature.

## For Sellers

Your home is more than square footage. It's the porch where you watched the sunsets, the yard where the kids learned to ride bikes, the kitchen that fed every holiday crowd. I'll price it honestly, present it beautifully, and market it to buyers who will appreciate what makes it special. Then I'll negotiate hard to get you the best result I can.

## For Buyers

Whether you're moving across the county or across the country, I'll help you understand this town the way a local does. I can tell you which neighborhoods catch the morning light, where you can walk to the water, and what life here is really like in January. My goal isn't just to find you a house. It's to make sure you love living here.

## Let's Talk

Thinking about buying, selling, or just curious what your home is worth? Give me a call or send a message. There's no pressure and no obligation. I'm happy to meet for coffee, take a drive around the lake, or answer your questions over the phone.

I look forward to helping you find your way home.
