**Subject: We're Now Open Saturdays, Plus a Few Other Updates**

Dear Friends of Ruiz Animal Clinic,

We know pets don't always get sick on a convenient schedule, and many of you have asked us to make it easier to get care outside the workweek. I'm happy to share that, beginning **March 1**, Ruiz Animal Clinic will be open on **Saturdays from 8:00 a.m. to noon**.

**What Saturday hours cover**

Saturday mornings are set aside for **sick visits and vaccines only**. We will not perform surgeries on Saturdays, and routine **wellness visits will continue to be scheduled Monday through Friday**.

Walk-ins are welcome and will be seen on a first-come, first-served basis. If your pet is **vomiting or limping**, please **call ahead** so our team can be ready for you when you arrive.

**Welcome, Dr. Tom Pak**

We're also excited to welcome **Dr. Tom Pak**, who joins our team from Pullman on **March 3**. Dr. Pak brings fresh energy and expertise to the clinic, and we can't wait for you and your pets to meet.

**Rabies clinic on March 15**

Mark your calendars for our rabies clinic on **March 15**. Rabies vaccines will be **$18 per shot, with no exam fee**. It's an easy, affordable way to keep your pet protected and up to date.

**New weekday lunch hour**

Please note that we now **close for lunch from 12:00 to 1:00 p.m. on weekdays**. We appreciate your patience as we make this change, and we encourage you to plan calls and drop-ins around that hour.

**After-hours emergencies**

If your pet has an emergency when we're closed, please call our after-hours emergency line at **509-555-0142**.

Thank you for trusting us with the care of your animals. Serving the pets and families of Yakima is a privilege, and we look forward to seeing you, now on Saturdays too.

Warm regards,

Dr. Ana Ruiz
