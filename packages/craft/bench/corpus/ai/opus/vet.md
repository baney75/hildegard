**Subject: New Saturday Hours at Ruiz Animal Clinic, Starting March 1**

Dear Friends,

We have some updates to share, including a new way to see us on the weekend.

**Saturday hours begin March 1**
Starting March 1, Ruiz Animal Clinic will be open Saturdays from 8:00 a.m. to noon. Saturday appointments are for sick visits and vaccines only. We will not perform surgeries on Saturdays.

Walk-ins are welcome and will be seen first come, first served. If your pet is vomiting or limping, please call ahead so we can be ready for you when you arrive.

Wellness visits will continue to be scheduled Monday through Friday.

**Welcome, Dr. Tom Pak**
We're pleased to announce that Dr. Tom Pak will join our team on March 3. Dr. Pak comes to us from Pullman, and we're excited for you and your pets to meet him.

**New weekday lunch closure**
We now close for lunch from 12:00 to 1:00 p.m. Monday through Friday. Please plan calls and drop-offs around this hour.

**Rabies clinic on March 15**
Join us on March 15 for our rabies clinic. Rabies vaccines will be $18 per shot, with no exam fee.

**After-hours emergencies**
If your pet has an emergency when we're closed, please call our after-hours emergency number: **509-555-0142**.

Thank you for trusting us with the care of your pets. We look forward to seeing you, on weekdays and now on Saturdays too.
