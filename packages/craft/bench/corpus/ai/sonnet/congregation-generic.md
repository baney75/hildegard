**Subject: Repairing Our Church Roof: What We Need and How We Will Pay for It**

Dear Friends,

Our church building has sheltered us through many seasons of worship, celebration, and mourning. Now it needs our help. A recent inspection confirmed that the roof has reached the end of its useful life. Water has begun to seep into the ceiling in several places, and if we wait, the damage will spread to the sanctuary walls, the wiring, and the floors. Repairing it now is both the faithful and the prudent thing to do.

**What the work involves**

The project will replace the worn roofing materials, repair the damaged decking underneath, and restore the flashing and gutters so that water drains away from the building. The Trustees have gathered bids from licensed contractors and recommend moving forward with the most thorough and best-value proposal. Work is expected to take several weeks and should be scheduled for the drier part of the season. We will keep services and activities going throughout, and we will share the schedule as soon as it is confirmed.

**How we will pay for it**

We want to be open about the cost and about our plan. We propose to fund the repair in three parts:

1. **Building reserve fund.** We will use the money our congregation has set aside over the years for major repairs. This is exactly what it was saved for, and it will cover the first portion of the cost.

2. **A special roof campaign.** We invite every household to make a one-time or pledged gift over the coming months. Pledges may be spread over up to twelve months to make giving easier. Gifts of every size matter, and no one will be asked to give beyond their means.

3. **Fundraising events and community support.** Our committees will organize a few gatherings, such as a community supper and a spring benefit sale, and we will apply for available grants for the preservation of historic and community buildings. Any amount raised this way reduces what we need from the campaign.

If the campaign and events fall short, the Trustees may seek a small, short-term loan to bridge the gap. We would much prefer to avoid borrowing, and our goal is to complete the project without debt.

**How you can help**

- Pray for our church, our leaders, and the workers who will be on our roof.
- Consider a gift or pledge. You can give by check, online through our website, or by placing an offering marked "Roof" in the plate.
- Volunteer for a fundraising event or help with outreach to neighbors and friends.
- Share this message with members who may not receive our emails.

We will post regular updates on progress toward our goal in the bulletin, in our newsletter, and during announcements on Sunday mornings. The Trustees and Finance Committee are always glad to answer questions, and the full bids and budget are available to any member who would like to review them.

Thank you for the generosity and care you have always shown this community. Together we will keep our building sound and our doors open for generations to come.

With gratitude,

Donovan Baney
On behalf of the Trustees and Church Council
