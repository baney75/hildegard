**Subject: Repairing Our Church Roof: What We Need and How We Will Pay for It**

Dear friends,

For many years, the roof over our sanctuary has sheltered our worship, our celebrations, our funerals, and our fellowship. Now it needs our care in return.

**What we have found**

Recent inspections confirmed that our roof has reached the end of its useful life. Worn shingles and failing flashing have allowed water to seep in, and left unaddressed, that damage will spread to the ceiling, the walls, and the structure beneath. Repairing it now will cost far less than waiting until the damage becomes worse. We plan to move forward with a full repair so that this building stays safe, dry, and welcoming for the generations who will come after us.

**How we will pay for it**

We want you to know exactly how we intend to fund this work, and we have built the plan in three parts:

1. **Our building fund.** We will first use the money already set aside for building maintenance. Thanks to the faithful giving of past members, we are not starting from zero.

2. **A special roof campaign.** We are inviting every household to prayerfully consider a one-time gift or a pledge spread over the coming months. Every gift matters, whether large or small. Contributions can be made through the offering plate, online, or by check, marked "Roof Fund."

3. **Community fundraising and outside support.** Our leadership will pursue available grants for historic and community buildings, and we will hold a few fellowship events, such as a meal and a community work day, where raising funds can also be a joy. If you have ideas or would like to help organize one, we would love to hear from you.

**Our commitment to you**

We will not take on debt that burdens the ministry of this church. We will share regular updates on the progress of the campaign and the repair work, and the full accounting of funds received and spent will be open to any member who wishes to see it.

**How you can help**

- Pray for the project, the workers, and the generosity of our community.
- Give what you are able, in the way that suits you best.
- Volunteer your time or share your ideas for fundraising.
- Tell others who love this church and may want to take part.

A church is more than its building, but this building has been the place where our faith has been lived out together. Let us take care of it as we would any home that has held us close.

Thank you for your faithfulness, your generosity, and your love for this community.

In gratitude and hope,

Your Church Leadership
