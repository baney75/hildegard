Dear Families,

Thank you for trusting us with your children. Caring for them is a joy, and we are grateful for the partnership we share with each of you. I'm writing to let you know about a change to our pick-up policy, which takes effect on **Tuesday, December 1, 2026**.

**What is changing**

Our daily closing time remains 5:30 p.m. Starting December 1, a late fee of $15 will be charged for the first 10 minutes after closing, and $1 per minute after that. Until now, we have handled late pick-ups informally, and we have not charged for them.

**Why we are making this change**

Late pick-ups have become more frequent this fall. When a child is still here after hours, it affects the evening routine for our own family, and it can be hard on the child, who is often tired and ready to go home. A clear policy lets us keep the day predictable and fair for everyone.

**What this means for you**

- Please plan to arrive by 5:30 p.m. so that you have time to gather belongings and say goodbye.
- If you know you will be delayed, please call or text us as early as you can. We understand that traffic and emergencies happen, and we will use our judgment for true emergencies.
- Late fees are due with your next regular payment.
- If your schedule has changed and you need a later pick-up time on a regular basis, please talk with us. We may be able to arrange an extended-hours option.

We know that changes like this are never convenient, and we have not made this decision lightly. Our goal is simply to keep our days calm and our children well cared for.

If you have questions or concerns, please speak with us at drop-off or pick-up, or send a message anytime. We are always glad to talk it through.

Thank you for your understanding and for being such a wonderful part of our daycare family.

Warmly,
Your Home Daycare Providers
