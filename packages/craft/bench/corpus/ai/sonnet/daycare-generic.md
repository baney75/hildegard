Dear Families,

Thank you for trusting us with your children. Caring for them each day is a privilege, and we want to keep our home a safe, warm, and well-run place for every child. To help with that, we are making a change to our sick-child policy, effective Monday, December 1.

**What is changing**

Starting December 1, a child must be fever-free for 24 hours without fever-reducing medication (such as Tylenol or ibuprofen) before returning to daycare. The same 24-hour rule applies after vomiting or diarrhea. Until now, we have asked for a shorter waiting period, and we have found it is not long enough to keep illness from spreading.

**Why we are making this change**

In a home daycare, children share toys, tables, and close space all day. When one child returns too soon, others often get sick, and so do our own family members. Those illnesses mean missed workdays for you and closed days for us. A consistent 24-hour rule gives everyone a clear standard and helps keep our group healthier through the winter months.

**What this means for you**

- Please keep your child home if they have a fever of 100.4°F or higher, have vomited, or have had diarrhea.
- If your child becomes ill during the day, we will call you right away. Please plan to pick up within one hour.
- Please let us know by text if your child is staying home, and tell us the reason, so we can watch for patterns and notify other families when needed.
- If your child is on antibiotics for a contagious illness, they may return after 24 hours of treatment and once they are fever-free.

**A note on fairness**

We know how hard it is to arrange backup care on short notice, and we do not make this change lightly. We will apply the policy equally to every family, including our own. If you have concerns about coverage or about how this affects your schedule, please talk with us. We are glad to work through it together.

A signed copy of the updated policy is attached to this letter. Please sign and return it by Friday, November 21.

Thank you for your understanding and your partnership. Your support helps keep all of our children healthy and happy.

With appreciation,

Donovan Baney
Home Daycare Provider
