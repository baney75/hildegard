# We're Moving! Okafor Family Dentistry Is Heading to Stellhorn Road

After many years on North Wells Street, Okafor Family Dentistry is moving to a new home. On **January 12**, we will open our doors at **6115 Stellhorn Rd.** in Fort Wayne.

Dr. Samuel Okafor has cared for families in this community since 2004, and this move lets us do that better. Here is what the new office offers:

- **Six treatment chairs, up from four.** More chairs means more flexibility in our schedule and less waiting for you.
- **A wheelchair-accessible entrance.** We want every patient to be able to walk, roll, or be wheeled into our office with ease.
- **Parking for 30 cars.** Plenty of room, so arriving for your visit is simple.

## Important Dates
