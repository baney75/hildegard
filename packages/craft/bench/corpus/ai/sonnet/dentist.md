# We're Moving! Okafor Family Dentistry Is Heading to Stellhorn Road

After many years on North Wells Street, Okafor Family Dentistry is moving to a new home. On January 12, we will open our doors at **6115 Stellhorn Rd.** in Fort Wayne.

Dr. Samuel Okafor has cared for patients in this community since 2004, and this move is about continuing that care with more room and easier access for everyone who walks through our door.

## What's New

- **Six treatment chairs, up from four.** More chairs mean more flexibility in our schedule and shorter waits for appointments.
- **A wheelchair-accessible entrance.** Our new office is designed so that every patient can enter comfortably and with ease.
- **Parking for 30 cars.** Plenty of space right at our door, so arriving for your visit is simple.

## Important Dates

- **January 5 to 9:** Our office will be closed while we move.
- **January 12:** We open at our new location, 6115 Stellhorn Rd.

If you need care before the closure, please call us soon so we can find a time that works for you.

## Your Appointments Are Safe

Every appointment scheduled for January 12 or later stays exactly as booked. You don't need to call or rebook. Just come to our new address at the time you already have.
