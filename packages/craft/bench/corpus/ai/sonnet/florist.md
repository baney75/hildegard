# About Petal & Pine

Petal & Pine is a flower shop on Center Street in Rutland, Vermont, set up in a former hardware store. We opened in 2012, and we like to think the building still has a bit of its old purpose: people come here when they need something made well and made right.

## What we grow, and where the rest comes from

Every summer and into fall, we grow dahlias and zinnias on two acres in Pittsford. They go from field to bucket to bouquet without traveling far, and you can see the difference in how they hold up and how they look. For everything else, we buy from a co-op in Brattleboro, so even the flowers we don't grow come to us from Vermont.

We don't sell artificial flowers. Nadine, the owner, doesn't work with them, and we'd rather send you home with something living.

## Who we are

Two full-time designers, Josh and Leah, make the arrangements you see in the shop and the ones that go out the door. At the holidays, three part-time teammates join us to keep up with the season.

## Weddings

Weddings book about nine months out, so if you have a date, get in touch early. We'll talk through what you're picturing and what's growing, and build from there.

## Sympathy flowers

Last year we made 1,400 funeral arrangements. It's work we take seriously, and we know that when you call us, it's rarely an easy day. We'll take care of the details so you don't have to.

## Delivery

We deliver within 15 miles of the shop, Tuesday through Saturday, for $12.

## Visit us

Stop by Center Street in Rutland, or get in touch to place an order or talk about an event. We'd love to make something for you.
