# About Petal & Pine

Petal & Pine is a flower shop on Center Street in Rutland, Vermont. We opened in 2012 in a former hardware store, and we still like to think the building has kept some of its purpose. People come here when they need something built well and on time.

I'm Nadine Fournier, the owner. Josh and Leah, our two full-time designers, make everything you see in the shop. At the holidays, three part-time designers join us to keep up with the season.

## Where our flowers come from

Two acres in Pittsford are given over to dahlias and zinnias, which we grow ourselves and bring in fresh through the season. The rest of our flowers come from a co-op in Brattleboro, so even the stems we don't grow are sourced from Vermont growers.

We work only with fresh flowers. I don't do artificial flowers, and you won't find them here.

## Weddings

Weddings book about nine months out. If you're engaged and have a date, get in touch early, and we'll plan the flowers around the day you're imagining.

## Funerals and sympathy

Last year we made 1,400 funeral arrangements. It is a large part of what we do, and we take it seriously. When a family trusts us with flowers for someone they've lost, we want the arrangement to be right.

## Delivery

We deliver within 15 miles of the shop, Tuesday through Saturday, for $12.

## Visit us

Come by the shop on Center Street in Rutland. We'd be glad to see you.
