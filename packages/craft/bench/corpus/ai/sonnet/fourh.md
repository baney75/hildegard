# Clover Creek Champions
### November Newsletter · Bremen, Indiana

---

## A Bigger, Stronger Club

Welcome to November, Champions! We're thrilled to share that our club has grown to **23 members** this year, up from 17 last year. That's a wonderful jump, and it shows how much energy there is around Clover Creek. To our new members and their families: we're so glad you're here.

## Congratulations, Hannah!

At the Marshall County Fair in July, Hannah Yoder's Hampshire gilt took **Reserve Grand Champion**. Hannah's hard work, patience, and care in the barn paid off in a big way, and the whole club is proud of her. Well done, Hannah!

## Pecan Fundraiser a Success

Thank you to everyone who sold, bought, and delivered in this year's fall fundraiser. Together we sold **410 pounds of pecans** and cleared **$1,180**. That money will pay for our spring trip to the state fairgrounds, so every order helped get us closer to the road.

## December Meeting: New Date and Location

Please note the change! Because the fire station hall is being repainted, our December meeting will be held:

- **When:** Tuesday, December 9, at 7:00 p.m.
- **Where:** Bremen Public Library

Please mark your calendars and pass the word to anyone who might otherwise head to the fire station.

## Volunteers Needed: State Fairgrounds Trip

We need **two parent volunteers to drive** on our spring trip to the state fairgrounds. If you can help, please let a club leader know at the next meeting. Drivers make this trip possible, and we'd be grateful for your help.

---

Thank you for another great season, Champions. See you in December!
