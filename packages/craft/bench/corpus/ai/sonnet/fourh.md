# Clover Creek Champions 4-H Club
## November Newsletter · Bremen, Indiana

### A Bigger Club This Year

Clover Creek Champions has grown. We have **23 members** this year, up from 17 last year. That's a big jump, and it shows in our meetings. Welcome to all our new members and their families. We're glad you're here.

### Reserve Grand Champion at the Fair

Congratulations to **Hannah Yoder**, whose Hampshire gilt won **reserve grand champion** at the Marshall County Fair in July. Hannah put in the work all season, and the result shows it. We're proud of you, Hannah!

### Pecan Fundraiser a Success

Our fall fundraiser is wrapped up, and the numbers are good. Members sold **410 pounds of pecans** and cleared **$1,180**. That money will pay for our spring trip to the state fairgrounds. Thank you to every member who sold, and to every neighbor, relative, and friend who bought.

### December Meeting: New Date and Place

Please note the change. The December meeting will be held on **Tuesday, December 9, at 7 p.m.** at the **Bremen Public Library**. The fire station hall is being repainted, so we can't meet there this time. We'll be back at our usual spot after the new paint dries.

### Drivers Needed for the State Fairgrounds Trip

We need **two parent volunteers** to drive on our spring trip to the state fairgrounds. If you can help, please let a club leader know at the next meeting or reach out to us before then. Every ride makes the trip possible for our members.

Thank you for another great season, Clover Creek families. See you in December!

*Clover Creek Champions 4-H Club · Bremen, Indiana*
