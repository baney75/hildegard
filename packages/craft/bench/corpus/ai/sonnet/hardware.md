# Our Story

Becker Hardware has been on the same ground in Hastings, Nebraska, since 1955, when my grandfather, Walt Becker, borrowed $2,000 and opened the doors. He had a small stock, a lot of determination, and a belief that people should be able to walk into a store and get what they need from someone who knows what it's for.

That idea hasn't changed.

## What we sell

We carry paint, plumbing supplies, and fasteners by the pound, so you can buy exactly six screws or exactly six hundred. We cut keys. We cut glass. If you bring in a broken part and a question, someone here will help you figure out what to do next.

## How we've grown

In 1982, my dad added the garden center, and it has been part of the store ever since. Spring still starts here a little early every year.

## The flood

In 2019, floodwater put four feet of water in our basement. It was a hard stretch. Then the neighbors showed up with shop vacs. Customers, friends, people we'd known for years and people we'd only nodded to at the counter all pitched in. We reopened because of them, and we haven't forgotten it.

## The people

Today there are 11 of us working here. Doris has been at the register for 31 years, and there's a good chance she has rung up your parents, too. Stop in and say hello.

## What we're not

We're not trying to compete with the big box stores on price, and we won't pretend otherwise. What we offer is time, knowledge, and a staff that knows your name. When you're in the middle of a project and need advice, that tends to be worth more than a few cents saved.

## Come see us

We're open Monday through Saturday, 7:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Come by, bring your questions, and let us help you get the job done.

Ray Becker
