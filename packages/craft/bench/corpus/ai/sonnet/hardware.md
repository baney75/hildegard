# Our Story

Becker Hardware has been on the same corner in Hastings, Nebraska, since 1955. That was the year my grandfather, Walt Becker, borrowed $2,000 and opened the doors. He didn't have much more than a plan to be useful to the people around him, and that plan has held up for seventy years.

## What we do

We sell paint, plumbing parts, and fasteners by the pound, so you can buy exactly the five screws you need or a coffee can full of them. We cut keys. We cut glass. If you walk in with a broken part in your hand and a puzzled look, someone here will help you figure out what it is and what replaces it.

## Growing up in the aisles

In 1982 my dad added the garden center, and it has been a spring ritual for many Hastings families ever since. I grew up in this store, and now I run it. Three generations of the family have worked these floors, and I'm still learning things from the people around me.

## The flood

In 2019, floodwater put four feet of water in our basement. It was a bad stretch. What I remember most is how many neighbors showed up with shop vacs, ready to work. Customers and friends pitched in without being asked. We reopened because the town helped us do it, and we don't forget that.

## The people

We have 11 employees. Doris has worked our register for 31 years, and there's a good chance she has rung up your parents, too. When you check out here, you're often greeted by name.

## What we're not

We aren't trying to beat the big box stores on price, and we won't pretend otherwise. What we offer is different: staff who know the products, the quantity you actually need, and a store that knows your town because it's part of it.

## Come in

We're open Monday through Saturday, 7:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Stop by, tell us what you're working on, and we'll help you get it done.

Ray Becker
Becker Hardware, Hastings, Nebraska
