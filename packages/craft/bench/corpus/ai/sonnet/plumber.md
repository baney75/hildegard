# About Dobbs Plumbing and Drain

I'm Carl Dobbs, and I've been fixing Waco's pipes since 2004. Before I started this business, I spent eleven years as a journeyman plumber for a big outfit. I learned the trade there, and I also learned what I didn't want to do: send customers a bill that doesn't match what they were told. So when I opened my own shop, I built it around doing honest work at a price you know up front.

## Who You'll Meet

Dobbs Plumbing and Drain is a small crew. It's me, Luis, and Danny, and when you call, you'll likely hear my wife, Pat, on the other end of the line. She keeps the schedule straight and makes sure the right plumber gets to your door. You won't be bounced through a call center or handed off to a stranger.

## What We Do

We focus on three things and do them well:

- **Repairs.** Leaks, failed fixtures, running toilets, low pressure, and the other problems that crop up in a home.
- **Water heaters.** In winter we fix about six a week, so we know them inside and out, whether yours needs a repair or a replacement.
- **Drain cleaning.** Slow drains, stubborn clogs, and backups, cleared and sorted out.

We don't do new construction. We stick to repair and service work, which means we can give your home our full attention.

## Flat-Rate Pricing

Before I start any job, I'll tell you what it costs. It's a flat rate, quoted ahead of time. If I find something unexpected, I'll talk to you before anything changes. No hourly clock running, and no surprise at the end.

## Here When Pipes Burst

Pipes don't wait for business hours. We're on call for burst pipes at any hour, day or night. If water is where it shouldn't be, call us and we'll get there.

## Straight Advice

I won't sell you a repipe you don't need. If a repair will solve the problem, that's what I'll recommend, even when a bigger job would pay me more. If a repipe is truly the right answer, I'll show you why. My name is on the truck, and I want you to call us again, and tell your neighbors, because we treated you fairly.

If you've got a plumbing problem in Waco, give us a call. Pat will pick up.
