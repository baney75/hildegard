# About Dobbs Plumbing and Drain

I started Dobbs Plumbing and Drain in Waco in 2004. Before that, I spent eleven years as a journeyman plumber for a big outfit. I learned the trade there, and I also learned what I didn't want my own business to look like. I wanted to be the person who shows up, tells you the price, and does the job right.

## Who You'll Deal With

We're a small shop. There's me, Luis, and Danny, and my wife Pat answers the phone. When you call, you get Pat. When we show up, you get one of the three of us, not a stranger sent from a dispatch center.

## What We Do

We do repairs, water heaters, and drain cleaning. We don't do new construction. That's a choice. It lets us put our time into the problems homeowners actually have: the leak under the sink, the clogged main line, the water heater that quit on a cold morning.

Winter is busy for water heaters. In the cold months we fix about six a week, so we've seen just about every way one can fail, and we usually know what we're looking at when we walk in.

## Flat-Rate Pricing

Before I start any job, I quote you a flat rate. That's the price you pay. You won't get a surprise on the invoice because the work took longer than expected or because we found something "extra" once the wall was open.

## Burst Pipes at Any Hour

Pipes don't burst on a schedule. We're on call for burst pipes at any hour, day or night. If water is coming through your ceiling at 3 a.m., call, and Pat or one of us will pick up.

## Straight Answers

I won't sell you a repipe you don't need. Replacing the plumbing in a house is sometimes the right call, but it's a big expense, and plenty of problems can be fixed with a lot less. If a repair will solve your problem, that's what I'll recommend. If the pipes really are at the end of their life, I'll tell you that too, and show you why.

That's how I've run this business since 2004, and it's how we'll treat you.
