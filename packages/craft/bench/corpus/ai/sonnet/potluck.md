**Harvest Potluck: Sunday, October 26**

Join your St. Brendan's family for our annual Harvest Potluck on Sunday, October 26, immediately following the 10:30 Mass in the parish hall. Father Tom will bless the food before we eat, and then it's time to feast and enjoy one another's company.

The Knights of Columbus are smoking two briskets for the occasion. Everything else is up to you! Please bring a dish to share.

**Please include a card listing your dish's ingredients.** We have two children with nut allergies, and your card helps keep them safe and lets every family eat with confidence.

To help us avoid a repeat of last year's nine pans of green beans, please sign up on the sheet in the narthex and let us know what you plan to bring. Last year 140 people joined us, and we ran out of plates by noon, so we're planning ahead this time. Feel free to bring a few extras if you can.

We hope to see you there!
