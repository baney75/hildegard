**Harvest Potluck: Sunday, October 26**

Join your St. Brendan's family for our annual Harvest Potluck on Sunday, October 26, right after the 10:30 Mass in the parish hall. Father Tom will bless the food, and then we eat.

The Knights of Columbus are smoking two briskets, so the main course is covered. We're counting on you for everything else. Please bring a dish to share, along with a card listing its ingredients. We have two children with nut allergies, and your card helps keep them safe.

Last year 140 people came, and we ran out of plates by noon. This year we're planning ahead, but you're welcome to bring a few extras if you can.

To avoid a repeat of the nine pans of green beans, please sign up on the sheet in the narthex and tell us what you plan to bring.

Come hungry, come glad, and come ready to share a meal together.
