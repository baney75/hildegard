Privacy policy

Last updated: [Owner: date of approval]. Applies to Hildegard, the app (version 0.1.x), and to Hildegard’s skills, wherever they are listed.

The short version

  • Hildegard runs on your own computers. What you make, your prompts, your voices and your settings stay there.
  • The skills collect nothing. They are text files that an AI app reads.
  • The app has no analytics, no tracking and no account with the people who make it. It sends nothing to Donovan Baney or to any server he runs.
  • The app reaches other companies’ servers only for the jobs in the table below. Some happen by default (checking for updates, installing, downloading a model you chose). The rest happen only when you turn them on.

The skills

Hildegard’s skills are instructions: Markdown files, a few small scripts and some page templates. They run inside the AI app you put them in, under that app’s rules and its own privacy policy. They contain no code that sends anything to Donovan Baney or to anyone else, and listing them in a store (Claude’s directory, OpenAI’s plugin directory, Grok Bot) does not change that. What an AI app does with your conversation is between you and that app.

The app

Hildegard is free, open-source software (AGPL-3.0-only). You install it, it runs a small server on your computer that only you can reach (the loopback address, with Host and Origin checks on every request), and it does its work on your own graphics cards. Your films, stills, voices, 3D files, projects and settings live in a data folder on your computer. Passwords you set for people you invite are stored as salted scrypt hashes; keys and tokens (Hugging Face, Runpod, Figma, tunnel credentials, the keys other devices use) go to your system’s keychain, not to files or logs.

What Donovan Baney receives

Nothing from the app or the skills. There is no telemetry, no crash reporter and no account. If you open an issue or a pull request on GitHub, what you write there is public and is held by GitHub. If you email [Owner: contact email], the message and your address are kept to answer you.

Your choices

  • Turn off automatic updates in Settings, or run the app offline after install; every render works offline once the models are downloaded.
  • Revoke any app, key or device in Settings › Sharing, or with hildegard host keys revoke and hildegard choir revoke.
  • Turn off a link or tunnel with hildegard host off or hildegard tunnel off.
  • Delete your data by deleting Hildegard’s data folder and its entries in your keychain.
