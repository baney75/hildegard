Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026. Applies to Hildegard, the app (version 0.1.x), and to Hildegard’s skills, wherever they are listed.

The short version

  • Hildegard runs on your own computers. What you make, your prompts, your voices and your settings stay there, unless you turn on a service that sends them elsewhere: a rented GPU, a link or tunnel, an AI app, or a model address you add. The table below lists each one.
  • The skills collect nothing and send nothing to us. They are text files and a few small scripts that an AI app reads and runs for you. Some of those scripts rely on programs you install yourself, and installing or first running those downloads files from other companies’ servers, only when you run that tool. Each one is in the table below.
  • The app has no analytics, no tracking and no account with the people who make it. It sends nothing to Donovan Baney or to any server he runs.
  • The app and the skills’ tools reach other companies’ servers only for the jobs in the table below. Some happen by default (checking for updates, installing, downloading a model you chose). The rest happen only when you turn them on.

The skills

Hildegard’s skills are instructions: Markdown files, a few small scripts and some page templates. They run inside the AI app you put them in, under that app’s rules and its own privacy policy. Two of their scripts can open a network connection, both listed in the table below: motion.py, for a font it has not cached yet, and cutout.py, whose rembg library downloads its model the first time. Nothing in them sends anything to Donovan Baney. The page templates load nothing from outside: no web fonts, scripts, stylesheets or images from another site. Listing the skills in a store (Claude’s directory, OpenAI’s plugin directory, Grok Bot) does not change any of that. What an AI app does with your conversation, and with any web search or page it opens for you because a skill asked it to, is between you and that app and its own policy.

What the skills do ask for is the programs their helper scripts and instructions use. Those are not included. If you or your AI app install or first run one, that program downloads from its own source. The last rows of the table below list every one the skills name with an install command or a download, and when it happens.

The app

Hildegard is free, open-source software (AGPL-3.0-only). You install it, it runs a small server on your computer that only you can reach (the loopback address, with Host and Origin checks on every request), and it does its work on your own graphics cards. Your films, stills, voices, 3D files, projects and settings live in a data folder on your computer. Passwords you set for people you invite are stored as salted scrypt hashes; keys and tokens (Hugging Face, Runpod, Figma, tunnel credentials, the keys other devices use) go to your system’s keychain, not to files or logs.

What Donovan Baney receives

Nothing from the app or the skills. There is no telemetry, no crash reporter and no account. If you open an issue or a pull request on GitHub, what you write there is public and is held by GitHub. If you email support@dbaney.com, the message and your address are kept to answer you.

This website

dbaney.com/hildegard is a static page: no cookies, no advertising and no analytics scripts. The install page remembers which operating system tab you picked, in your own browser’s local storage, and sends it nowhere. It is served by Cloudflare, which logs a sample of about one request in ten (the address, the page asked for, the time and the browser it named) and keeps those logs for at most 7 days. They are used only to keep the site running.

Your choices

  • Turn off automatic updates in Settings, or run the app offline after install; every render works offline once the models are downloaded.
  • Revoke any app, key or device in Settings › Sharing, or with hildegard host keys revoke and hildegard choir revoke.
  • Turn off a link or tunnel with hildegard host off or hildegard tunnel off.
  • Delete your data by deleting Hildegard’s data folder and its entries in your keychain.
