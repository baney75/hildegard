Questions

Do you hold my money?

No. Clients pay into your own bank account or your own payment provider. Fairbill only reads the rate and records the payment.

Which rate do you use?

The European Central Bank’s reference rate on the day the payment arrives, or the rate your bank actually gave you if you type it in.

Can my accountant get in?

Yes, with a read-only link that you can switch off. They see invoices and exports, never your settings.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your account drops to Solo. Every invoice stays, readable and exportable; you just can’t add a fourth client.
