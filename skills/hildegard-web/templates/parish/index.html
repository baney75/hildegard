Catholic parish of Harbor Falls · Diocese of Marston

Whoever you are, there is a place for you at this table.

We are a parish of fishermen’s families, students from the college and new neighbours from four continents. Come as you are; the doors open half an hour before every Mass.

Confession and the sacraments

Confession
Saturdays 3:30 to 4:30 p.m., or ring the parish office for a time that suits you.
Baptism
On the second Sunday of the month after the 10:30. Parents meet Deacon Tomás once beforehand.
Marriage
Please speak to Father Kofi at least six months before the date you have in mind.
The sick and housebound
Call the office at any hour. A priest will come, and Communion is brought every Friday.
Funerals
Your funeral director will contact us; you may also call us first and we will help with everything.

Giving

Everything here, from the heating to the pantry, is paid for by people in the pews. Last year you gave $214,600; $38,900 of it went straight to the pantry and the night shelter.

Give in the basket, by envelope, or by standing order to St. Brendan Parish, account 0041 7731. Ask the office about giving in memory of someone.
