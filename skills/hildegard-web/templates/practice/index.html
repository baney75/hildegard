What we treat

  • Check-ups and cleaning

    Every six months, forty minutes, with an X-ray once a year if you need one.

  • Fillings and crowns

    White fillings as standard. Crowns are made in our own lab in three days.

  • Children from age one

    Saturday mornings are for children only, with a quiet room for the nervous ones.

  • Toothache, today

    Call before 10 and we keep a slot for you the same day, every weekday.

  • Nervous patients

    Longer appointments, a stop signal you choose, and sedation when it helps.

  • Dentures and repairs

    Relines and repairs while you wait, most in under two hours.

Your first visit

  1. Before you come

    We text you a short health form. Bring a list of any medicines you take.

  2. Twenty minutes to talk

    Dr. Halvorsen or Dr. Mbeki asks what brought you in and what you want from us. No treatment yet.

  3. A careful look

    An exam, and X-rays only if they’ll change what we do. We show you what we see on the screen.

  4. A written plan

    Every option and its price, in writing, before anything starts. Take it home and think.

Out of hours

For a knocked-out tooth, swelling that spreads to your eye or neck, or bleeding that won’t stop, go to Hartwell General’s emergency room. For pain that can wait until morning, leave a message on our line and we call you back by 8:15.
