## Icons and sacred image traditions

- **The Sinai Pantocrator** (6th century, Saint Catherine's Monastery); **Andrei Rublev**, *The Trinity* (1420s); the **Monreale** and **Cefalù** Pantocrator mosaics. Cefalù's open Gospel is bilingual, in Greek and Latin, and both sites' tesserae run in concentric rings around the figure.
- **Dionysius of Fourna, *Hermeneia*** (the painter's manual of Mount Athos, about 1730s): the canon for icon painting.
- **Willson, "The ho Ōn Inscription in Christ's Halo" (2021):** the cruciform halo's Ὁ ὬΝ, "He Who Is," from the Septuagint of Exodus 3:14.
- **The icon's color grammar:** the red chiton of divinity, the blue himation of the humanity He put on; gold assist lines along fold ridges.
- **Augustine, *Confessions* 7.9:** the Platonists had "In the beginning was the Word," but not "the Word was made flesh."

## Music video

Directors to study, and what to take from each:
- **Michel Gondry:** structure mapped to the instruments ("Star Guitar," "Around the World").
- **Spike Jonze:** characters, not the filmmaker, should seem to have made it. Sendak's advice to him was to be "personal and dangerous."
- **Hiro Murai:** intuitive, not overthought; he listens to a song 100 to 200 times while writing.
- **Chris Cunningham:** the uncanny, fused with sound.
- **Jonathan Glazer:** a single relentless idea.
- **Floria Sigismondi;** **Kahlil Joseph:** collage, and the album as film.
- **Hype Williams, Dave Meyers:** spectacle and color.
- **Mark Romanek:** archival intercut ("Hurt").
- **Steve Barron:** the a-ha "Take On Me" rotoscope.
- **Stephen R. Johnson with Aardman:** "Sledgehammer."
- **OK Go's** one-take videos.
- **The visual-music lineage:** Fischinger, Lye, McLaren, Reiniger (`music-video.md`).

## Agent skills learned from (GitHub)

- **anthropics/skills:**
  - `algorithmic-art` writes an algorithmic philosophy before any p5.js, with seeded randomness for reproducible pieces;
  - `canvas-design` names a movement and a design philosophy first, and treats text as a minimal accent;
  - `slack-gif-creator` encodes destination limits (size, frames, colors) as hard rules;
  - `frontend-design` names the common AI-design looks to avoid.

  *Taken into Hildegard:* philosophy (the seed and the lens) before marks; destination limits as hard constraints.
- **pbakaus/impeccable:**
  - one core skill with domain reference files;
  - explicit lists of negative rules;
  - a single holistic gate ("would someone immediately guess AI made this?");
  - diagnosis (critique for meaning; audit for technical quality) kept separate from repair;
  - named repair verbs (distill, bolder, quieter, polish).

  *Taken into Hildegard:* the reference-file architecture, the slop test, the critique/audit split, and the revision verbs (SKILL.md step 6).
- **remotion-dev skills:** video as code with exact frame timing. Used through the Remotion skills when present.
